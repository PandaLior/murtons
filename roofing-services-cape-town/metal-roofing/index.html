Types of Metal Roofing We Install

Murtons Projects installs comprehensive metal roofing solutions for Cape Town properties:

IBR Sheeting (Inverted Box Rib)

IBR roofing is Cape Town's most popular metal roofing profile, featuring wide trapezoidal ribs providing excellent strength and modern clean appearance. IBR sheets span greater distances reducing purlins needed, offer superior water shedding with wide valleys, suit low-pitch roofs (minimum 5 degrees), provide contemporary aesthetics, and install quickly.

IBR Benefits:

  • Modern clean profile suits contemporary architecture
  • Excellent structural strength allows wider spacing
  • Superior water drainage prevents ponding
  • Suitable for low-pitch applications
  • Quick installation reduces labour costs
  • Available in Chromadek or Colorbond
  • Various colours including Slate Grey, Charcoal, Sandstone
  • Ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings

Corrugated Iron Roofing

Corrugated iron features traditional wavy profile providing classic appearance popular for rural properties, traditional homes, and agricultural buildings. Narrower corrugations create distinctive look. Corrugated roofing requires steeper pitch (minimum 10 degrees) but offers proven performance and cost-effective pricing.

Corrugated Iron Benefits:

  • Traditional classic appearance
  • Cost-effective economical pricing
  • Proven performance over decades
  • Suitable for various building types
  • Easy repairs and maintenance
  • Widely available materials
  • Good for outbuildings, garages, sheds

Chromadek Steel Roofing

Chromadek is galvanized steel with chromate treatment providing enhanced corrosion resistance. Available in IBR or corrugated profiles, Chromadek offers good durability at reasonable cost. Standard Chromadek comes unpainted (silver metallic finish) or factory-painted in various colours. Chromadek performs well in most Cape Town locations including moderate coastal exposure.

Chromadek Features:

  • Galvanized steel with chromate treatment
  • Good corrosion resistance for most areas
  • Available painted or unpainted
  • Cost-effective pricing (R350-R550/m²)
  • 25-35 year lifespan with maintenance
  • Suitable for residential and commercial
  • Can be repainted after 10-15 years

Colorbond Steel Roofing

Colorbond represents premium pre-painted steel roofing offering superior performance, especially in coastal areas. Colorbond features advanced five-layer coating system with superior corrosion resistance, UV-stable colours that won't fade, excellent durability (35-50 years), and modern aesthetic appeal. More expensive than Chromadek but provides better longevity, appearance, and performance.

Colorbond Benefits:

  • Premium five-layer protective coating
  • Superior coastal corrosion resistance
  • UV-stable colours maintain appearance
  • 35-50 year lifespan
  • Wide colour range (15+ colours)
  • Heat-reflective lighter colours
  • Lower maintenance requirements
  • Ideal for premium homes and coastal properties
  • Comprehensive manufacturer warranty

Popular Colorbond Colours: Surfmist (lightest), Shale Grey, Windspray, Woodland Grey, Basalt, Monument, Evening Haze, Ironstone, and more.

Professional Metal Roof Installation Process

Murtons Projects follows systematic installation procedures ensuring quality metal roofing:

1. Site Assessment and Measurement

We conduct comprehensive roof assessment measuring roof dimensions and pitch, evaluating roof structure and purlins, assessing wind exposure and load requirements, and planning sheet layout minimizing waste. Accurate measurements ensure correct material quantities and efficient installation.

2. Material Selection and Quote

We provide detailed quotes specifying material type (IBR vs corrugated), material grade (Chromadek vs Colorbond), colour selection, sheet thickness, quantities needed, and complete pricing including materials, fixings, flashings, labour, and installation. We explain options helping you choose materials suited to your property type, budget, coastal exposure, and aesthetic preferences.

3. Roof Structure Preparation

We prepare the roof structure installing or inspecting purlins (horizontal support beams), ensuring proper spacing for sheet profile, confirming structural adequacy, and preparing for sheeting installation. Proper purlin spacing is critical for sheet support and wind resistance.

4. Underlayment Installation (if required)

For residential applications or when installing over existing decking, we install roofing felt or breathable membrane providing additional waterproofing protection, condensation control, and thermal benefits. Commercial installations may use exposed purlins without underlayment.

5. Metal Sheet Installation

Our experienced installers lay metal sheets systematically starting from eaves working toward ridge, ensuring correct overlap between sheets (minimum one corrugation), proper side lap (150mm minimum), secure fixing with appropriate screws and washers, and straight alignment maintaining appearance. Sheets are cut accurately for valleys, hips, and roof penetrations.

6. Fixing and Fastening

We use quality roofing screws with neoprene washers providing weatherproof sealing. Screws are placed at correct spacing (every second corrugation), driven into purlin centers, tightened properly (not overtightened), and sealed preventing water penetration. Proper fixing is critical for wind resistance in Cape Town's conditions.

7. Flashing Installation

We install comprehensive flashings at all critical areas including ridge flashing at roof apex, eaves flashing at roof bottom, barge flashing at gable ends, valley flashing where slopes meet, wall flashings at walls and parapets, and penetration flashings (vents, chimneys, pipes). Quality flashings prevent water penetration maintaining weatherproof integrity.

8. Ridge Capping and Ventilation

Ridge capping covers roof apex with ventilated ridge designs allowing airflow reducing heat buildup and condensation. Proper ventilation is essential for metal roof performance, preventing moisture accumulation and improving thermal comfort.

9. Gutter Integration

We integrate metal roofing with gutter systems ensuring proper overhang, secure gutter attachment, and effective water drainage. Gutters and downpipes handle Cape Town's intense winter rainfall. See our drainage services for gutter installation.

10. Final Inspection and Cleanup

We conduct thorough quality inspection checking secure fixing throughout, proper sheet overlap, effective water drainage, neat professional appearance, and weatherproof sealing. Complete site cleanup removes all offcuts, packaging, and debris. Your property is left clean ready to enjoy your new metal roof.

Benefits of Metal Roofing for Cape Town Properties

Metal roofing offers numerous advantages making it increasingly popular:

Lightweight Construction

Metal roofing weighs only 5-10 kg per m² compared to 40-50 kg/m² for tiles. This lightweight construction reduces structural load, allows installation on existing structures, requires less substantial roof framing, and enables quicker installation with less labour.

Excellent Wind Resistance

Properly installed metal roofing withstands Cape Town's powerful winds including strong south-easters. Large sheets with secure fixing create wind-resistant surface. Metal roofing performs excellently in coastal areas with high wind exposure when correctly specified and installed.

Quick Installation

Metal roofing installs much faster than tiles, typically completing in 1-3 days for residential projects. Large sheets cover area quickly, reducing labour time and costs. Quick installation minimizes weather exposure risk and property disruption.

Cost-Effective Value

Metal roofing provides excellent value at R350-R550 per m² for standard materials (cheaper than tile roofing). Lower installation costs due to quick installation, suitable for various budgets, and excellent performance per rand invested make metal roofing cost-effective choice.

Suitable for Low-Pitch Roofs

IBR sheeting works on low-pitch roofs (minimum 5 degrees) where tiles aren't suitable. This versatility suits modern flat-look designs, extensions with limited height, and commercial buildings with shallow roof slopes.

Wide Colour Selection

Modern metal roofing offers extensive colour choice including light reflective colours (reducing heat), dark contemporary colours (charcoal, black), earth tones (brown, terracotta), and neutral greys. Colorbond UV-stable colours maintain appearance for decades.

Low Maintenance

Metal roofing requires minimal maintenance. Occasional cleaning, rust treatment if needed, and potential repainting after 15-20 years keeps metal roofs performing well. Much less maintenance than painted surfaces or high-maintenance materials.

Long Lifespan

Quality metal roofing lasts 30-40 years (Chromadek) or 35-50 years (Colorbond) when properly installed and maintained. Excellent longevity provides decades of reliable weather protection.

Versatile Applications

Metal roofing suits diverse property types including modern homes, commercial buildings, industrial facilities, warehouses, agricultural buildings, garages and outbuildings, extensions and additions, and coastal properties. Versatility makes metal roofing popular across Cape Town.

Metal Roofing for Coastal Areas

Cape Town's coastal areas including Bloubergstrand, Milnerton, Sea Point, Camps Bay, and Hout Bay require special roofing considerations due to salt air corrosion. Metal roofing performs excellently in coastal zones when properly specified:

  • Use Colorbond: Superior corrosion resistance ideal for coastal exposure
  • Proper Overlaps: Prevent salt accumulation in gaps
  • Quality Fixings: Stainless steel screws with neoprene washers
  • Regular Washing: Remove salt deposits preventing corrosion
  • Prompt Repairs: Address scratches or damage immediately
  • Professional Installation: Ensure weatherproof integrity

Frequently Asked Questions About Metal Roofing

How much does metal roofing cost in Cape Town?

Metal roofing costs in Cape Town range from R350-R550 per m² for standard IBR or corrugated iron installation, and R500-R700 per m² for premium Colorbond steel roofing. A typical 150m² home costs R52,500-R82,500 for standard metal roofing or R75,000-R105,000 for Colorbond. Costs include sheeting, fixings, flashings, labour, and installation. Final pricing depends on roof size, pitch, material choice (Chromadek vs Colorbond), roof complexity, and access. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for accurate metal roofing quotes.

What's the difference between IBR and corrugated roofing?

IBR (Inverted Box Rib) sheeting features wide trapezoidal ribs providing greater strength and spanning capacity, modern clean appearance, better water shedding, and is ideal for low-pitch roofs (minimum 5 degrees). Corrugated iron has traditional wavy profile, narrower corrugations, classic appearance, and requires steeper pitch (minimum 10 degrees). Both perform excellently in Cape Town's climate. IBR is more popular for modern homes and commercial buildings, while corrugated suits traditional and rural properties. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 to discuss which suits your property.

How long does metal roofing last in Cape Town?

Metal roofing lasts 30-40 years in Cape Town when properly installed and maintained. Chromadek (galvanized with chromate treatment) lasts 25-35 years, while premium Colorbond with superior coating technology lasts 35-50 years. Lifespan depends on material quality, coating thickness, coastal proximity (salt exposure), maintenance (repainting every 10-15 years), and installation quality. Coastal properties benefit from Colorbond's superior corrosion resistance. Regular maintenance including cleaning and treating rust extends lifespan. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for durable metal roofing.

Is metal roofing suitable for coastal areas in Cape Town?

Yes, metal roofing performs excellently in Cape Town's coastal areas when proper materials and installation are used. Colorbond steel roofing offers superior corrosion resistance ideal for salt air exposure in areas like Bloubergstrand, Milnerton, Sea Point, and Camps Bay. Chromadek also works well but requires more frequent maintenance. We recommend Colorbond for properties within 5km of ocean, proper overlaps to prevent salt accumulation, regular washing to remove salt deposits, and quality screws with neoprene washers. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for coastal metal roofing solutions.

Can metal roofs be installed over existing roofs?

Sometimes metal roofing can be installed over existing roofs depending on structural capacity, existing roof condition, building code compliance, and proper ventilation planning. We assess existing roof structure ensuring adequate support for additional weight, check for damage requiring removal, ensure proper deck condition, and plan ventilation preventing condensation. Installing over existing roofs saves removal costs but isn't always advisable. We provide honest assessment of whether overlay installation is suitable for your property. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for professional evaluation.

Does metal roofing make homes hot in Cape Town summers?

Modern metal roofing with proper installation doesn't cause excessive heat when correctly specified. We use light-colored Colorbond with heat-reflective properties reducing heat absorption, install proper roof insulation (ceiling insulation is essential), create adequate roof ventilation preventing heat buildup, and use radiant barriers when needed. Light colors reflect more heat than dark colors. Proper ceiling insulation is most critical factor for temperature control. Metal roofs with insulation perform comparably to tile roofs for thermal comfort. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 to discuss heat management solutions.
