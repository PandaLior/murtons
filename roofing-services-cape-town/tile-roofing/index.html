Types of Tile Roofing We Install

Murtons Projects installs a comprehensive range of roofing tiles suited to Cape Town properties:

Concrete Tile Roofing

Concrete tiles manufactured from cement, sand, and pigments provide excellent durability at affordable pricing. Modern concrete tiles offer superb performance with improved strength, colour stability, and weather resistance. Available in multiple profiles and colours, concrete tiles suit most architectural styles from contemporary to traditional.

Popular Concrete Tile Profiles:

  • Double Roman: Classic curved profile with distinctive shadow lines, Cape Town's most popular tile style
  • Marseille: Traditional French-style tile with elegant curved profile, suits heritage and traditional homes
  • Slate Profile: Flat appearance mimicking natural slate, modern aesthetic for contemporary designs
  • Flat Tiles: Clean minimal profile for modern architectural styles

Concrete Tile Benefits:

  • 40-50 year lifespan with proper maintenance
  • Cost-effective pricing (R450-R650 per m² installed)
  • Wide colour range including terracotta, brown, charcoal, grey
  • Good fire resistance
  • Excellent thermal insulation
  • Lower weight than clay tiles
  • Easy replacement if damaged

Clay Tile Roofing

Clay tiles represent premium natural roofing with exceptional longevity and timeless beauty. Made from fired natural clay, these tiles improve with age, developing rich patina adding character to homes. Clay tiles suit heritage properties, Tuscan-style homes, Mediterranean designs, and premium estates throughout Constantia, Bishopscourt, and upmarket suburbs.

Clay Tile Benefits:

  • 50+ year lifespan, often lasting 100+ years
  • Natural material that ages beautifully
  • Superior thermal properties (cooler in summer)
  • Excellent fire resistance
  • Timeless aesthetic appeal
  • Environmentally friendly natural product
  • Low maintenance requirements
  • Premium property appearance

While clay tiles cost more initially (R550-R850 per m²), their exceptional lifespan and superior performance provide excellent long-term value for quality-focused homeowners.

Slate Profile Tiles

Slate profile tiles (concrete) provide the appearance of expensive natural slate at fraction of the cost. These flat profile tiles suit modern homes, contemporary designs, and properties seeking clean lined aesthetics. Available in charcoal, black, and grey colours, slate profile tiles create sophisticated roofscapes popular in upmarket developments.

Professional Tile Roof Installation Process

Quality installation determines tile roof performance and longevity. Murtons Projects follows systematic installation procedures ensuring lasting results:

1. Roof Assessment and Measurement

We conduct thorough roof inspection measuring roof area and slopes, assessing roof structure and trusses, identifying any structural concerns, and evaluating site access and logistics. Accurate measurements ensure correct material quantities preventing delays.

2. Material Selection and Quote

We provide detailed quotes specifying tile profile, colour options, material quantities, underlayment specifications, batten requirements, and complete pricing. We explain options helping you choose tiles suited to your property, budget, architectural style, and estate requirements. Call 074-141-1494 for your tile roofing quote.

3. Roof Structure Preparation

For new roofs, we inspect and prepare the roof deck ensuring solid substrate, replacing any damaged boards, confirming structural adequacy for tile weight, and preparing for underlayment installation. Tile roofs require proper structural support due to their weight (approximately 40-50kg per m² for concrete tiles).

4. Underlayment Installation

We install quality breathable underlayment (roofing felt or modern membrane) providing critical waterproofing layer beneath tiles. Underlayment installation includes full deck coverage with proper overlap, secure fixing to prevent wind lift, special attention to valleys and hips, and integration with flashings. Proper underlayment is essential for Cape Town's winter rainfall, providing backup waterproofing if tiles are displaced or damaged.

5. Batten Installation

We install treated timber battens and counter battens at precise spacing determined by tile profile and roof pitch. Counter battens running vertically create ventilation gap beneath battens, promoting airflow preventing moisture buildup. Horizontal battens support tiles at manufacturer-specified spacing ensuring proper tile overlap and alignment. All battens are securely fixed to trusses using appropriate fasteners.

6. Tile Installation

Our skilled roofers install tiles systematically from eaves to ridge with proper overlap between courses, correct side overlap per tile profile, alignment maintaining straight coursing, and secure fixing using tile clips and screws in wind-exposed areas. Eaves course uses special starter tiles, verge tiles at roof edges, and valley tiles for internal valleys. Installation proceeds efficiently with quality control throughout.

7. Ridge and Hip Installation

Ridge capping and hip capping secure roof apex and hip lines. We install ridge tiles using flexible bedding compound (not rigid cement), secure fixing preventing wind lift, proper overlap between ridge tiles, and bird-proof combs preventing nest building. Modern flexible compounds accommodate thermal expansion preventing cracking and tile displacement.

8. Flashing and Waterproofing Details

Critical waterproofing includes valley flashings, wall and parapet flashings, chimney and skylight flashings, and roof penetration sealing (vents, pipes). Quality flashings prevent water penetration at vulnerable roof junctions. We use durable metal flashings or modern flexible flashing systems suited to tile roofing.

9. Final Inspection and Cleanup

We conduct thorough quality inspection verifying proper tile alignment, secure fixing throughout, effective drainage, and professional finish. Complete site cleanup removes all debris, old materials, and packaging. Your property is left pristine ready to enjoy your beautiful new tile roof.

Benefits of Tile Roofing for Cape Town Homes

Tile roofing offers numerous advantages making it ideal for Cape Town properties:

Exceptional Durability and Longevity

Tile roofs last 40-50 years for concrete tiles and 50+ years for clay tiles, significantly longer than most alternative roofing materials. Properly installed and maintained tile roofs often exceed these lifespans, providing decades of reliable protection. This longevity makes tiles excellent long-term investment despite higher initial cost.

Superior Fire Resistance

Tiles are non-combustible, providing excellent fire protection crucial for Cape Town properties. Tile roofs don't ignite from flying embers or direct fire exposure, protecting homes during fires and potentially reducing insurance premiums. Fire resistance is particularly important in areas prone to mountain fires like Hout Bay, Constantia, and Noordhoek.

Excellent Thermal Insulation

Tile roofs provide natural thermal insulation keeping homes cooler in Cape Town's hot summers. Air gap beneath tiles (created by batten system) provides additional insulation layer. Clay tiles offer superior thermal properties, significantly reducing heat transfer. Cooler homes mean reduced air conditioning costs and improved comfort.

Wind Resistance

Properly installed tiles withstand Cape Town's strong winds including powerful south-easters. Modern fixing systems using tile clips and screws secure tiles against wind lift. Interlocking tile profiles create wind-resistant surface. We follow SANS building codes for wind loading ensuring secure installation suited to your property's wind exposure.

Low Maintenance Requirements

Tile roofs require minimal maintenance compared to painted surfaces or metal roofing. Occasional cleaning removes moss and debris. Individual tiles can be replaced if damaged without affecting surrounding areas. No painting required, though roof coating can refresh appearance after decades. Proper maintenance maximizes the already impressive tile roof lifespan.

Timeless Aesthetic Appeal

Tile roofs provide classic, elegant appearance suiting Cape Town's diverse architectural styles. From Mediterranean villas to contemporary homes, tiles enhance property aesthetics and street appeal. Wide colour range allows matching estate requirements or personal preferences. Tiles never look dated, maintaining visual appeal for decades.

Environmental Benefits

Tiles are environmentally responsible roofing choice. Clay tiles use natural renewable material. Concrete tiles use readily available materials. Both are recyclable at end of life. Long lifespan reduces replacement frequency minimizing environmental impact. Natural thermal properties reduce energy consumption for cooling.

Excellent Property Value

Quality tile roofs enhance property value and appeal to buyers. Buyers recognize tile roof quality, longevity, and low maintenance. Well-maintained tile roofs are significant selling point. Investment in quality tile roofing returns substantial value at sale time.

How much does tile roofing cost in Cape Town?

Tile roofing costs in Cape Town range from R450-R650 per m² for concrete tiles to R550-R850 per m² for clay tiles, fully installed. How long do tile roofs last in Cape Town?

Tile roofs last 40-50 years for quality concrete tiles and 50+ years for clay tiles when properly installed and maintained. Lifespan depends on tile quality, installation standards, maintenance (cleaning, moss removal), Cape Town's climate exposure, and proper underlayment and waterproofing. Tiles themselves rarely fail completely; typically underlayment or flashings need replacement after 20-30 years. Regular maintenance maximizes roof longevity. What's the difference between concrete and clay tiles?

Concrete tiles are manufactured from cement, sand, and pigments, offering good durability at lower cost (R450-R650/m²), 40-50 year lifespan, various profiles and colours, and require periodic maintenance. Clay tiles are natural fired clay providing premium appearance, 50+ year lifespan, improve with age, superior thermal properties, higher cost (R550-R850/m²), and classic Mediterranean aesthetics. Both perform excellently in Cape Town's climate. Choice depends on budget, architectural style, and preferences. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for recommendations.
