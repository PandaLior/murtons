Our Roofing Services in Cape Town

Comprehensive roofing solutions for residential and commercial properties

Roof Repairs Cape Town

Professional roof repair services for all roof types and roofing problems. We fix roof leaks, replace broken or cracked tiles, repair storm damage, address wind damage, fix ridge capping, replace fascia boards and soffits, and repair roof trusses. Our experienced team diagnoses the root cause of roofing issues and provides lasting repairs using quality materials. Fast response times with same-day service available for urgent repairs. Suitable for tile roofs, metal roofs, slate roofs, and flat roofs throughout Cape Town.

Roof Replacement & Re-Roofing

Complete roof replacement services for aged, damaged, or deteriorating roofs. We handle full re-roofing projects including roof structure assessment, old roof removal, roof deck inspection and repair, new roof installation, and proper waterproofing. Ideal for roofs over 20-25 years old, extensively damaged roofs, or when upgrading to better roofing materials. We work with all roof types and provide detailed quotes with material options suited to your budget and Cape Town's climate. Minimal disruption with professional project management.

Tile Roofing Installation

Specialist tile roofing services for Cape Town homes. We install concrete tiles (including double Roman, Marseille, slate profile) and clay tiles with proper underlayment and battens. Tile roofs offer excellent durability (40-50+ year lifespan), fire resistance, thermal insulation, and traditional aesthetics. Perfect for residential properties in Constantia, Durbanville, and heritage areas. We also repair tile roofs, replace broken tiles, and re-bed ridge tiles. All tile work includes proper ventilation and waterproofing for Cape Town's winter rainfall.

Metal Roofing Solutions

Metal roofing installation using IBR sheets, corrugated iron, and Chromadek steel roofing. Metal roofs are ideal for modern homes, industrial buildings, commercial properties, and coastal areas due to excellent wind resistance, lightweight structure, quick installation, and cost-effectiveness. Available in various colours with Colorbond coating for UV resistance and longevity. Perfect for flat or low-pitch roofs and areas requiring large spans. We also repair existing metal roofs, replace corroded sheets, and apply rust treatment and protective coatings.

Flat Roof Installation & Repair

Flat roof solutions for modern homes, extensions, and commercial buildings. We install and repair flat roofs using torch-on waterproofing, EPDM rubber membrane, fibreglass (GRP), and liquid rubber systems. Flat roofs require proper waterproofing and drainage to handle Cape Town's winter rainfall. Our flat roofing services include roof deck preparation, insulation installation, waterproof membrane application, and parapet wall waterproofing. Ideal for Sea Point apartments, commercial buildings, and contemporary architectural designs. See our waterproofing services for more details.

Emergency Roof Repairs

24/7 emergency roofing services for urgent roofing problems throughout Cape Town. We respond quickly to severe roof leaks, storm damage, wind damage, falling trees, fire damage, and structural damage. Our emergency team provides temporary repairs to prevent further damage, followed by permanent repairs when weather permits. Available for residential and commercial properties within our 30km service area from Milnerton. Keep our number handy: 074-141-1494 for immediate emergency roofing assistance.

Commercial Roofing Services

Commercial roofing specialists for businesses, offices, warehouses, industrial buildings, retail properties, and multi-unit residential complexes. We handle large-scale roofing projects with minimal business disruption, working outside business hours when needed. Our commercial services include metal roofing, flat roof systems, roof maintenance programs, roof inspections, and emergency repairs. Fully insured with public liability coverage. Experienced with building compliance, safety regulations, and project management for commercial properties throughout Cape Town's business districts.

Residential Roofing Solutions

Residential roofing services for Cape Town homes, from small cottages to large estates. We understand that your home is your biggest investment and treat every property with care and respect. Our residential services include all roof types, insurance claim assistance, colour consultation, weatherproofing recommendations, and respectful project management with clean daily site maintenance. Ideal for family homes in Durbanville, Table View, Constantia, and throughout the Northern Suburbs and Southern Suburbs.

Roof Waterproofing Services

Roof waterproofing using torch-on waterproofing, acrylic waterproofing, and membrane systems. Essential for flat roofs, parapet walls, and leak-prone areas. Waterproofing protects against Cape Town's winter rainfall and prevents water ingress, damp problems, and structural damage. We waterproof new roofs during installation and provide remedial waterproofing for existing leaking roofs. Also available for balconies, basements, and retaining walls. See our comprehensive waterproofing services page for detailed information.

Roof Painting & Coating

Roof painting services for tile roofs and metal roofs. Professional roof coating extends roof life, improves appearance, provides UV protection, and enhances waterproofing. We use premium roof paints with mold inhibitors, heat-reflective properties, and weatherproofing benefits. Our process includes high-pressure roof cleaning, moss and algae treatment, repairs to damaged areas, primer application, and two coats of quality roof paint. Ideal for refreshing aged roofs and protecting against Cape Town's sun exposure. See our painting services for more details.

Frequently Asked Questions About Roofing in Cape Town

How much does roof repair cost in Cape Town?

Roof repair costs in Cape Town typically range from R2,500 for minor repairs to R15,000+ for extensive work. The final cost depends on the roof type (tile, metal, flat), extent of damage, materials needed, and accessibility. Factors include whether it's a simple tile replacement, leak repair, or structural damage. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for a free, detailed quote tailored to your specific roofing needs.

Do you offer emergency roofing services in Cape Town?

Yes, Murtons Projects offers 24/7 emergency roof repair services throughout our 30km service area from Milnerton. We respond quickly to storm damage, severe leaks, wind damage, and urgent roofing emergencies affecting properties in Northern Suburbs, Atlantic Seaboard, and surrounding areas. Call 074-141-1494 immediately for emergency assistance.

What types of roofing do you install in Cape Town?

We install all major roof types including: concrete tiles, clay tiles, slate roofing, IBR metal sheets, corrugated iron, Chromadek steel roofing, flat roof systems, and pitched roofs. Our team has extensive experience with both residential and commercial roofing projects throughout Cape Town. We recommend roofing materials based on your property type, budget, and Cape Town's unique Mediterranean climate with strong winds and winter rainfall.

How long does a roof replacement take?

A typical residential roof replacement takes 3-7 days depending on the size, complexity, and weather conditions. A small home (100-150m²) might take 3-4 days, while larger properties or complex roof structures may take up to a week or more. Commercial roofing projects vary significantly based on building size. We provide detailed timelines during the free quote process and work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.

Do your roofing services come with a guarantee?

Yes, all Murtons Projects roofing work comes with a comprehensive 10-year workmanship guarantee. This industry-leading guarantee covers installation quality and workmanship. Additionally, roofing materials come with manufacturer warranties (typically 15-50 years depending on the product). We're fully insured with liability and workmen's compensation coverage for complete peace of mind. Contact us at 074-141-1494 for full guarantee details.

Can you fix roof leaks during winter in Cape Town?

Yes, we can perform emergency leak repairs during Cape Town's winter months, weather permitting. For permanent repairs and waterproofing work, we may need to wait for dry conditions. We offer temporary emergency repairs to prevent further damage during rainy periods, then complete permanent repairs when weather allows. Our team monitors weather forecasts carefully and communicates timing clearly. Call 074-141-1494 for immediate leak repair assessment.
