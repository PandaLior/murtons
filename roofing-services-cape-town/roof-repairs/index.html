Common Roof Problems We Repair

Our comprehensive roof repair services address all common and complex roofing problems affecting Cape Town properties:

Roof Leaks and Water Damage

Roof leaks are the most urgent roofing problem, causing water damage to ceilings, walls, insulation, and electrical systems. We locate leak sources using systematic inspection methods, then repair the underlying problem whether it's damaged tiles, faulty flashing, valley gutter failures, or waterproofing breakdown. Common leak locations include valleys, chimneys, skylights, roof penetrations, and parapet walls.

Broken, Cracked, and Missing Tiles

Tile damage from falling branches, storms, walking traffic, or age-related deterioration compromises roof weatherproofing. We replace broken tiles, cracked tiles, and missing tiles with matching profiles and colours. Our tile suppliers stock all major concrete tile profiles (Marseille, double Roman, slate profile) and clay tiles. For discontinued tiles, we source reclaimed matches or suggest tasteful replacement sections.

Storm Damage and Wind Damage

Storm damage from Cape Town's winter storms and summer south-easters can lift tiles, tear metal sheeting, break ridge cappings, and damage flashings. Our storm damage repair service includes temporary emergency repairs during bad weather, then comprehensive permanent repairs including re-securing tiles, replacing battens, fixing flashings, and reinforcing vulnerable areas. We assist with insurance claims providing documentation and working directly with assessors.

Valley Gutter Problems

Valley gutters channel high water volumes where two roof slopes meet, making them critical leak points. We repair corroded valley gutters, replace failed valley waterproofing, improve valley flashing, and install modern valley solutions including box valleys and pre-formed valley trays. Proper valley gutter repair prevents water penetration and ceiling damage.

Ridge Capping Repairs

Ridge capping secures roof apex tiles against wind lift and prevents water penetration along roof ridges. Over time, the cement bedding deteriorates, allowing tiles to shift or blow off. We provide ridge capping re-bedding using modern flexible compounds, replace damaged ridge tiles, and install bird proofing combs to prevent nest building under ridge tiles.

Flashing Failures

Roof flashings seal junctions between roofing and walls, chimneys, skylights, and other penetrations. Failed flashings cause persistent leaks. We replace corroded metal flashings, repair separated lead flashings, install proper chimney flashings, and upgrade to modern flashing systems including rubber-based flexible flashings for improved longevity.

Fascia Boards and Soffits

Rotten fascia boards and soffits result from prolonged water exposure, inadequate gutter maintenance, or roof overhang water runoff. We replace damaged timber fascias and soffits with treated timber or maintenance-free uPVC alternatives. Proper fascia repairs protect roof edges, support gutter systems, and maintain property aesthetics.

When You Need Roof Repairs

Don't delay roof repairs. Early intervention prevents minor problems from becoming major expenses. Contact us immediately if you notice:

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls indicating active or past leaks
  • Missing, broken, or cracked roof tiles visible from ground level
  • Damp spots in attics or roof spaces, especially after rain
  • Daylight visible through roof boards in attic spaces
  • Loose or missing ridge capping tiles
  • Sagging roof areas suggesting structural problems
  • Storm or wind damage with visible roof disruption
  • Moss, algae, or vegetation growth indicating moisture retention
  • Age-related deterioration on roofs over 20 years old
  • Interior mold or mildew suggesting hidden moisture problems

Regular roof inspections (annually or after major storms) catch problems early when repairs are simpler and less expensive.
