When You Need Roof Replacement

Knowing when to replace rather than repair your roof saves money and prevents ongoing problems. Consider roof replacement in these situations:

Age-Related Deterioration

Roofs have finite lifespans determined by material type and environmental conditions. Concrete tile roofs typically last 40-50 years, clay tiles 50+ years, metal roofing 30-40 years, and flat roof membranes 15-25 years. When your roof approaches or exceeds its expected lifespan with visible deterioration including widespread tile cracking, fading, moss buildup, or material breakdown, replacement becomes more cost-effective than repeated repairs.

Extensive Storm or Wind Damage

Cape Town's powerful south-easter winds and winter storms can cause severe roofing damage. When storm damage affects large roof areas with multiple missing tiles, lifted metal sheets, structural damage to battens or trusses, or compromised roof deck, replacement often costs less long-term than extensive repairs. Insurance may cover replacement when damage is severe enough.

Multiple Leak Points

A single leak usually warrants repair, but multiple leaks across different roof areas indicate broader problems like worn underlayment, general material failure, or systemic waterproofing issues. When leak repairs become frequent or widespread, complete replacement addresses underlying problems rather than repeatedly patching symptoms.

Costly Repairs

When repair costs exceed 40-50% of replacement cost, replacement makes financial sense. This occurs with extensive valley gutter failures, multiple roof sections needing work, significant structural repairs, or widespread tile replacement. A new roof with warranty provides better value than expensive piecemeal repairs.

Renovation and Upgrades

Major home renovations provide ideal timing for roof replacement. Adding a second story, reconfiguring roof lines, installing new ceilings, or upgrading insulation all benefit from simultaneous re-roofing. You can upgrade to better materials, improve energy efficiency, match new architectural styles, and coordinate work timing efficiently.

Material Upgrades

Sometimes homeowners choose replacement to upgrade from old, heavy concrete tiles to modern lightweight Colorbond metal roofing, switch tile colours or profiles for improved aesthetics, install premium clay tiles for heritage properties, upgrade to modern flat roof systems with better waterproofing, or improve thermal insulation and energy efficiency.

Structural Concerns

Visible roof sagging, cracked trusses, rotted roof deck boards, or structural movement indicate serious problems requiring immediate attention. These structural issues often necessitate roof replacement as part of comprehensive repairs to ensure building safety and code compliance.

Our Roof Replacement Process

Murtons Projects follows a systematic approach ensuring successful roof replacement projects:

1. Initial Consultation and Roof Inspection

Contact us at 074-141-1494 or request a quote online. We schedule a comprehensive roof inspection at your convenience. Our roofing specialist examines your existing roof condition, measures roof area and slopes, assesses roof structure and trusses, identifies any structural concerns, discusses your preferences and requirements, and photographs existing roof for documentation.

2. Detailed Quote and Material Selection

We provide a detailed written quote specifying work scope, recommended materials with options, accurate pricing for materials and labour, project timeline and schedule, and guarantee terms. We explain material choices suited to your property type, budget, architectural style, and Cape Town's climate. We source premium materials from reputable suppliers ensuring availability and consistency.

3. Project Planning and Scheduling

Once you approve the quote, we schedule your roof replacement coordinating with your availability and weather forecasts. We arrange material delivery to arrive on schedule, organize our experienced roofing crew, and coordinate waste disposal logistics. We communicate clearly about project timing, daily schedule, site access requirements, and weather contingencies.

4. Site Preparation and Protection

Before starting, we protect your property placing ground tarps to catch debris, protecting garden areas, shrubs, and landscaping, covering vehicles if necessary, securing site perimeter for safety, and setting up equipment and scaffolding safely. We inform neighbours about the project timing and minimize disruption.

5. Old Roof Removal

We carefully remove your old roof stripping all tiles or metal sheets systematically, removing old battens and counter battens, stripping old underlayment and waterproofing, and loading debris into waste containers. We protect exposed areas with tarps during removal. Old materials are disposed of responsibly at authorized facilities.

6. Roof Deck Inspection and Repairs

With the old roof removed, we thoroughly inspect the roof deck checking for rotten or damaged boards, examining roof trusses for damage or movement, identifying areas needing structural repairs, and assessing overall deck condition. We replace damaged roof boards, repair or reinforce trusses if needed, and ensure a solid substrate for the new roof. Significant structural problems are documented and discussed before proceeding.

7. Underlayment and Waterproofing

We install quality underlayment providing essential waterproofing barrier beneath tiles, using breathable membranes for tile roofs, or installing appropriate underlayment for metal roofing. We pay special attention to valleys, ridges, roof penetrations, and other critical waterproofing areas. Proper underlayment is crucial for Cape Town's winter rainfall.

8. Batten Installation (for Tile Roofs)

For tile roofing, we install treated timber battens and counter battens at proper spacing for your tile profile, secured with appropriate fixings to manufacturer specifications. Correct batten spacing is critical for tile alignment, wind resistance, and proper roof ventilation.

9. New Roof Installation

Our skilled roofers install your new roof following best practices and manufacturer guidelines. For tile roofing, we lay tiles with proper overlap and alignment, secure tiles with clips and fixings in high-wind areas, install ridge and hip tiles with flexible bedding compound, and ensure proper ventilation. For metal roofing, we install sheets with correct overlap, secure with appropriate screws and washers, install flashings and trims, and seal all penetrations properly.

10. Flashing and Detail Work

We install quality flashings at critical areas including valleys, walls and parapet walls, chimneys and skylights, roof penetrations (vents, plumbing), and eaves and verges. Proper flashing installation prevents water penetration at vulnerable roof junctions.

11. Final Inspection and Cleanup

We conduct thorough quality inspections checking all roof areas for proper installation, verifying secure fixing, testing drainage and water flow, and ensuring neat, professional finish. We then complete comprehensive site cleanup removing all debris and waste materials, cleaning gutters, sweeping driveways and paved areas, and restoring garden areas and landscaping. Your property is left clean and tidy.

12. Handover and Guarantee Documentation

We walk you through the completed roof explaining installation details, providing maintenance recommendations, presenting guarantee documentation (10-year workmanship), supplying material warranty information, and providing our contact details for any future questions. We ensure you're completely satisfied with your new roof.

Roofing Materials We Install

Murtons Projects installs all major roofing materials suited to Cape Town properties:

Concrete Tile Roofing

Concrete tiles are Cape Town's most popular residential roofing material offering excellent value, durability, and aesthetics. Available profiles include double Roman, Marseille, slate profile, and flat tiles. Benefits include 40-50 year lifespan, fire resistance, thermal insulation, wind resistance when properly fixed, various colours and finishes, and excellent value for money. Ideal for traditional homes, security estates, and standard residential properties. Cost: R450-R650 per m² installed.

Clay Tile Roofing

Clay tiles offer premium aesthetics and exceptional longevity. These natural tiles provide timeless appeal perfect for heritage homes, Tuscan-style properties, and premium estates. Benefits include 50+ year lifespan, natural material that improves with age, excellent fire resistance, superior thermal properties, distinctive appearance, and low environmental impact. Cost: R550-R850 per m² installed.

Metal Roofing (IBR and Corrugated)

IBR sheets and corrugated iron provide cost-effective, durable roofing ideal for modern homes, commercial buildings, and industrial properties. Benefits include lightweight (reduces structural load), quick installation (saves time), excellent wind resistance, long lifespan (30-40 years), suitable for low-pitch roofs, available in Chromadek or Colorbond with various colours, and cost-effective pricing. Perfect for coastal areas, modern architectural styles, and large-span buildings. Cost: R350-R550 per m² installed.

Colorbond Steel Roofing

Colorbond is premium pre-painted steel roofing offering superior corrosion resistance, UV-stable colours that won't fade, modern aesthetic appeal, and excellent performance in coastal areas. More expensive than standard Chromadek but provides better longevity and appearance. Popular for high-end homes and properties near the ocean.

Flat Roof Systems

For flat roofs or low-pitch roofs on modern homes, extensions, and commercial buildings, we install torch-on waterproofing (modified bitumen), EPDM rubber membrane, fibreglass (GRP) roofing, and liquid rubber systems. These systems provide excellent waterproofing for flat or low-slope roof applications. See our flat roof solutions page for detailed information.

How much does roof replacement cost in Cape Town?

Roof replacement costs in Cape Town vary based on roof size, materials, and complexity. Typical costs: concrete tile roofs R450-R650 per m², clay tile roofs R550-R850 per m², metal roofing (IBR/corrugated) R350-R550 per m², and flat roof systems R500-R900 per m². A typical 150m² home costs R67,500-R97,500 for tile roofing or R52,500-R82,500 for metal roofing. Final costs include old roof removal, deck repairs, new materials, labour, and waterproofing. How long does a roof replacement take?

Typical residential roof replacement takes 3-7 days depending on size and complexity. A small home (100-150m²) takes 3-4 days, medium homes (150-200m²) take 4-5 days, and larger properties (200m²+) take 5-7 days or more. Timeline factors include roof size, material type (tiles take longer than metal), weather conditions, structural repairs needed, and roof complexity. Commercial projects vary by building size. When should I replace my roof instead of repairing it?

Consider roof replacement when: your roof is 20-25+ years old with widespread deterioration, repairs would cost more than 40-50% of replacement cost, you have multiple leak areas across the roof, significant structural damage to trusses or deck, extensive storm or fire damage, you're renovating and want to upgrade materials, or insurance recommends replacement.
