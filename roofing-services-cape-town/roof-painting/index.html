Roof Painting Services

We provide professional roof painting for all suitable roof types throughout Cape Town:

Tile Roof Painting

Tile roof painting transforms faded concrete and clay tiles restoring vibrant colour and weather protection. Tiles become porous with age absorbing moisture, growing moss and algae, and showing UV damage. Professional painting seals surfaces, prevents biological growth, and delivers fresh appearance lasting 8-12 years.

We paint all tile profiles including double Roman, Marseille, slate profile, flat tiles, and pantiles. Concrete tiles respond excellently to painting with dramatic appearance transformation. Clay tiles can be painted though some owners prefer natural aging patina. Our painting process includes thorough tile cleaning, crack and damage repairs, primer specifically formulated for porous tile surfaces, and two coats of premium acrylic or silicone roof paint in your chosen colour.

Metal Roof Painting

Metal roof painting protects IBR, corrugated steel, and Chromadek roofing from rust, corrosion, and UV deterioration. Metal roofs benefit from painting providing rust prevention and treatment, UV protection preventing paint chalking and fading, heat reflection reducing interior temperatures, restored appearance eliminating weathering and discoloration, and extended roof lifespan through corrosion protection.

Metal roof preparation includes thorough cleaning, rust treatment with inhibiting primers, minor rust repairs, leak repairs at fasteners or seams, and metal-specific primer application. We apply two coats of premium metal roof paint or specialized heat-reflective coatings. Metal roof painting is particularly valuable for coastal Cape Town properties where salt exposure accelerates corrosion.

Roof Cleaning Services

High-pressure roof cleaning removes years of accumulated moss, algae, lichen, dirt, and pollution without painting. Cleaning alone dramatically improves roof appearance and removes biological growth retaining moisture and causing tile deterioration. Many homeowners choose cleaning-only service for newer roofs not requiring painting yet.

Professional roof cleaning costs R35-R50 per m² significantly less than complete painting. We use appropriate pressure settings preventing tile damage, apply biocide treatments killing moss and algae, and ensure thorough debris removal. Roof cleaning every 3-5 years maintains appearance and prevents growth-related deterioration. Cleaning is essential preparation before roof painting ensuring paint adhesion.

Ridge Capping Re-bedding

Ridge capping re-bedding secures loose ridge tiles preventing wind lift and water penetration. Aging cement bedding deteriorates allowing tiles to shift. We re-bed ridge cappings using modern flexible compounds during roof painting projects ensuring complete roof restoration. Ridge re-bedding costs R450-R650 per linear meter and should be included with painting projects if ridge tiles are loose.

Roof Repairs Before Painting

Professional roof painting includes necessary repairs before painting ensuring roof integrity and paint performance. We replace broken or cracked tiles, repair damaged sections, fix minor leaks and flashings, and address all structural concerns. Painting over damage wastes money creating attractive roof that still leaks or deteriorates. Honest assessment determines if roof condition justifies painting or requires more extensive repairs or replacement.

Roof Painting Process

Our systematic approach ensures quality lasting roof painting results:

1. Roof Inspection and Assessment

We thoroughly inspect your roof assessing overall condition and suitability for painting, identifying repairs needed before painting, checking ridge capping condition, evaluating moss and algae severity, and discussing colour preferences and options. Inspection determines if painting is appropriate or if other solutions (repairs, replacement) better serve your needs. We provide honest recommendations even if painting isn't suitable. Call 074-141-1494 to schedule your free roof painting assessment.

2. Detailed Quotation

We provide comprehensive written quotes specifying complete scope including cleaning, repairs, painting, itemized pricing for each component, paint brand and specifications, colour choices available, timeline and weather considerations, and guarantee terms. Detailed quotes ensure understanding of complete service avoiding surprise costs.

3. Colour Consultation

We help you select roof colours considering home architectural style and existing colours, estate or neighborhood requirements, personal preferences, heat reflection properties (lighter colours reflect more heat), and availability in premium paint brands. We provide colour samples and examples from previous projects. Popular Cape Town roof colours include terracotta, charcoal, nutmeg brown, slate grey, and burgundy.

4. Property Protection and Setup

Before work begins, we protect gardens, paving, vehicles, and property from cleaning debris and paint overspray, cover gutters preventing debris entry, establish debris collection systems, and set up safety equipment and access. Proper protection prevents property damage during roof painting.

5. High-Pressure Roof Cleaning

Thorough professional cleaning removes all moss, algae, lichen, and biological growth, dirt, dust, and pollution accumulation, old loose paint if repainting, and ensures completely clean surface for primer adhesion. We use appropriate pressure preventing tile damage and apply biocide treatments killing remaining spores. Roof must be completely dry before painting - we monitor weather ensuring dry conditions.

6. Roof Repairs and Ridge Re-bedding

We complete all necessary repairs identified during inspection including replacing broken or cracked tiles, fixing damaged areas, addressing minor leaks, and re-bedding loose ridge capping. Repairs ensure roof integrity before investing in painting. Quality preparation determines painting longevity and performance.

7. Primer Application

Primer application is critical for paint adhesion and longevity. We apply primers specifically formulated for roof surfaces (porous tile primers, metal primers, etc.), ensure complete coverage of all surfaces, allow proper drying time before top coats, and use quality primer brands ensuring paint system compatibility. Skipping primer compromises paint adhesion causing premature failure.

8. Paint Application - Two Top Coats

We apply two complete top coats of premium roof paint ensuring thorough coverage, consistent colour, adequate paint thickness for protection, proper drying time between coats, and attention to difficult areas (ridges, valleys, edges). Two coats are essential for durability and appearance - single-coat applications compromise longevity. Premium acrylic or silicone-based roof paints provide excellent UV resistance, weatherproofing, and heat reflection.

9. Quality Inspection and Cleanup

Completed work undergoes thorough quality inspection verifying complete coverage, consistent colour and finish, proper ridge and detail work, and professional appearance. We conduct complete property cleanup removing all debris, equipment, and materials, cleaning gutters of debris, restoring property condition, and ensuring your satisfaction. You receive maintenance guidelines maximizing paint lifespan.

Benefits of Professional Roof Painting

Quality roof painting delivers substantial benefits for Cape Town homeowners:

Cost-Effective Roof Restoration

Roof painting costs R80-R120 per m² compared to R450-R850 per m² for roof replacement. A 150m² home costs R12,000-R18,000 for painting versus R67,500-R127,500 for replacement - saving R50,000-R110,000 while achieving similar appearance transformation. Painting is economically sensible for structurally sound roofs with cosmetic deterioration.

Extended Roof Lifespan

Professional painting extends roof lifespan 8-12 years by sealing porous surfaces preventing water absorption, protecting against UV deterioration, preventing moss and algae growth causing damage, and reducing thermal cycling stress. Painting 15-20 year old roofs can achieve 25-35 year total lifespan delaying expensive replacement.

Improved Energy Efficiency

Light-coloured roof paints and heat-reflective coatings reduce roof surface temperatures by 10-20°C reflecting solar radiation rather than absorbing it. Cooler roofs mean cooler homes reducing air conditioning costs and improving comfort. Heat-reflective benefits are particularly valuable for Cape Town's hot summer months. Silicone-based paints offer superior heat reflection.

UV Protection

Cape Town's intense sun causes UV deterioration of roof tiles and paint. Quality roof paint provides UV-resistant barrier preventing tile surface breakdown, colour fading and chalking, and premature aging. UV protection is essential for roof longevity in South Africa's high UV environment.

Weather Protection and Waterproofing

Roof paint seals porous tile surfaces reducing water absorption during Cape Town's winter rains, prevents freeze-thaw damage in higher altitude suburbs, resists wind-driven rain penetration, and maintains weatherproofing integrity. While not replacing proper waterproofing, paint contributes to overall weather resistance.

Moss and Algae Prevention

Painted roofs resist moss and algae growth better than unpainted surfaces. Quality roof paints include fungicides and algicides preventing biological growth. Prevents moisture retention that accelerates tile deterioration. Maintains clean attractive appearance longer between cleanings. Reduces maintenance requirements.

Enhanced Property Value and Curb Appeal

Professionally painted roofs dramatically improve home appearance and street appeal. Fresh roof transforms entire property appearance signaling well-maintained home to buyers. Increases property value and marketability. Particularly beneficial for homes being prepared for sale. Complements exterior house painting for complete property transformation.

How much does roof painting cost in Cape Town?

Roof painting costs in Cape Town range from R80-R120 per m² for complete professional service including high-pressure cleaning, repairs, primer, and two top coats of premium roof paint. A typical 150m² home costs R12,000-R18,000 for complete roof painting. How long does roof paint last in Cape Town?

Quality roof paint lasts 8-12 years in Cape Town conditions when properly applied over well-prepared surfaces. Lifespan depends on paint quality (premium brands last longer), surface preparation thoroughness, number of coats applied (two coats recommended), Cape Town's UV exposure and weather, roof orientation and sun exposure, and maintenance and cleaning. Acrylic roof paints offer good durability and UV resistance. Silicone-based paints provide superior heat reflection and longevity. Regular cleaning extends paint lifespan. Re-coating every 10-12 years maintains appearance and protection. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for long-lasting roof painting.

Can you paint all types of roofs?

We paint most roof types including concrete tile roofs (all profiles), clay tile roofs, metal roofs (IBR, corrugated, Chromadek), slate profile tiles, and fibre cement roofs. Different roof types require specific paint systems and preparation methods. Tile roofs need thorough cleaning and primer for porous surfaces. Metal roofs require rust treatment and metal-specific primers. Clay tiles can be painted though some prefer natural patina. Not all roofs are suitable for painting - we assess roof condition and advise honestly if painting is appropriate or if other solutions (replacement, repairs) are better options. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 to discuss painting your specific roof type.

Does roof painting prevent leaks?

Roof painting improves weather resistance and seals minor porosity but doesn't fix structural problems or major leaks. Roof paint benefits include sealing porous tile surfaces reducing water absorption, protecting against UV deterioration, preventing moss and algae growth retaining moisture, and extending overall roof lifespan. However, roof paint cannot fix broken tiles, cracked tiles requiring replacement, failed flashings or valley gutters, structural damage or sagging, or ridge capping failures. We repair all damage before painting ensuring roof integrity. Painting over problems without repairs wastes money and causes continued leaks. Honest assessment determines if painting is appropriate.
