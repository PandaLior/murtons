Roofing Materials for Panorama Homes

Based on Panorama's residential market and property types, we recommend:

Concrete Tiles

Most popular choice for Panorama family homes. Concrete roof tiles offer excellent value (R450-650 per m2 installed), good durability (25-30 years with maintenance), thermal insulation properties, traditional residential appearance, and wide color range matching neighborhood aesthetics. Available from trusted South African manufacturers including Braamfontein Roofing, Concrete Roof Tiles, and others. Perfect for replacing old tiles on established homes or new installations matching existing neighborhood character. Requires periodic sealing and painting for longevity.

Chromadek Steel Roofing

Popular for modern Panorama homes and renovations. Chromadek roofing provides cost-effective solution (R350-500 per m2 installed), lightweight construction reducing roof load, fast installation minimizing disruption, modern clean aesthetic, and excellent wind resistance. Available in various colors matching contemporary homes. Ideal for newer developments, older home conversions reducing weight, and properties requiring modern appearance. Lifespan of 20-30 years depending on coating quality and maintenance.

Clay Tiles

Premium option for higher-value Panorama properties. Clay roof tiles deliver superior longevity (40-60 years), excellent thermal properties, natural material aesthetic, low maintenance requirements, and premium appearance. More expensive (R650-900 per m2 installed) but provide best long-term value for family homes planning to stay long-term. Natural material doesn't fade or deteriorate like painted surfaces. Perfect for premium family homes and properties in upscale Panorama neighborhoods.

IBR Sheeting

Economical metal roofing for certain applications. IBR corrugated sheeting suits budget-conscious projects, carports and outbuildings, contemporary architectural designs, and faster installation timelines. Available in various colors and lengths. Less expensive than Chromadek but delivers good performance for appropriate applications.
