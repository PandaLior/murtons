Commercial Roofing Services

We provide comprehensive roofing services for commercial properties:

Commercial Metal Roofing Installation

Metal roofing dominates Cape Town commercial roofing for warehouses, factories, and large commercial structures. We install IBR (Inverted Box Rib) sheeting providing strength and coverage, corrugated steel sheeting for cost-effective applications, Chromadek pre-painted steel in various colours, Zincalume corrosion-resistant coating, and insulated metal panel systems improving energy efficiency.

Metal roofing benefits commercial properties through cost-effectiveness for large areas, long lifespan (20-40 years depending on coating), low maintenance requirements, fire resistance meeting building codes, rapid installation minimizing disruption, and excellent strength-to-weight ratio. Proper installation with appropriate fall, fixing, and flashing ensures decades of reliable performance.

Commercial Flat Roof Waterproofing

Flat roof waterproofing suits modern commercial architecture providing economical coverage, rooftop equipment accommodation, and future expansion flexibility. We install torch-on waterproofing (R450-R650/m²) for proven reliability, EPDM rubber roofing (R650-R800/m²) for premium longevity, liquid applied membranes for complex shapes, and GRP fibreglass for heavy traffic areas.

Commercial flat roofs require robust drainage systems handling Cape Town's winter rainfall. We design and install proper fall directing water to outlets, adequately sized drainage points and downpipes, overflow provisions for extreme weather, and regular maintenance access. Effective drainage prevents ponding and premature waterproofing failure. Learn more about our comprehensive flat roof solutions.

Commercial Roof Repairs and Maintenance

Commercial roof repairs address problems quickly minimizing business disruption and preventing escalation. Common repairs include leak repairs and waterproofing restoration, damaged or missing sheeting replacement, flashing repairs and upgrades, gutter repairs and replacement, structural repairs to purlins or trusses, and corrosion treatment and protective coatings.

Regular preventive maintenance extends commercial roof lifespan and prevents emergency failures. Our maintenance programs include scheduled inspections (bi-annual or quarterly), gutter cleaning and drainage maintenance, minor repairs before problems escalate, condition reporting and documentation, and planned replacement budgeting. Proactive maintenance is more cost-effective than reactive emergency repairs.

Commercial Roof Inspections

Professional roof inspections identify problems early when repairs are simple and inexpensive. We provide detailed inspection reports including comprehensive condition assessment, photographic documentation of issues, prioritized repair recommendations, cost estimates for required work, and remaining lifespan projections. Inspections support due diligence for property transactions, insurance requirements, and planned maintenance programs.

Complete Roof Replacement Projects

Commercial roof replacement becomes necessary when existing roofs reach end of life or no longer meet performance requirements. We manage complete replacement projects including detailed project planning and scheduling, safety plan development and implementation, phased work minimizing operational disruption, disposal of old roofing materials, installation of new roofing systems, and final inspection and documentation.

Large commercial roof replacement requires careful project management coordinating materials delivery, crew scheduling, weather monitoring, safety compliance, and client communication. Our experienced project managers ensure smooth execution meeting deadlines and budgets.
