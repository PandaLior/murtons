Roofing Materials for Century City Properties

Based on Century City's modern construction standards, we recommend:

Torch-On Waterproofing for Flat Roofs

Primary solution for Century City's flat roof buildings. Torch-on modified bitumen membranes offer excellent waterproofing performance (10-15+ year lifespan), heat-welded seams eliminating leak points, flexibility accommodating building movement, UV resistance for exposed applications, and proven reliability in Cape Town conditions. Available in APP (plastomeric) and SBS (elastomeric) formulations for different applications. Must be professionally installed by certified applicators - torch-on is not a DIY material. Ideal for commercial buildings, apartment blocks, and flat roof sections of residential properties.

Liquid Waterproofing Systems

Versatile option for complex roof shapes. Liquid polyurethane and acrylic coatings provide seamless waterproofing without joints, flexibility around penetrations and details, quick application for minimal disruption, excellent for maintenance and repairs, and UV-resistant topcoats in various colors. Suitable for residential properties, complex roof geometries, maintenance applications, and areas requiring seamless finishes. Professional application ensures proper thickness and performance.

Chromadek Metal Roofing

Popular choice for Century City residential properties. Chromadek pre-painted steel offers modern aesthetic matching contemporary architecture, lightweight construction suitable for modern building methods, good wind resistance, cost-effective performance, and wide color range matching development aesthetics. Requires proper insulation for thermal comfort and condensation control. Ideal for townhouses, residential complexes, and contemporary homes. Must include proper ventilation preventing condensation issues common in modern sealed buildings.

Modern Concrete Tiles

Traditional option for some Century City residential areas. Quality concrete tiles provide good thermal properties, traditional aesthetic where required, durability in Cape Town weather, and wide color selection. Heavier than Chromadek requiring adequate structural support. Suitable for residential complexes requiring more traditional appearance while maintaining modern performance standards. Proper installation and waterproofing underlays are essential.

Materials to Avoid

Inferior waterproofing products (fail prematurely causing expensive damage), DIY waterproofing systems (improper application leads to leaks), standard bitumen without proper reinforcement (degrades quickly), and uninsulated metal roofing (condensation and thermal issues) should be avoided in Century City's modern developments.
