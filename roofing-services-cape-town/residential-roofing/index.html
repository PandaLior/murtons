Residential Roofing Services for Cape Town Homes

Murtons Projects provides comprehensive roofing services for all residential roofing needs:

New Roof Installation

New roof installation for home additions, extensions, garages, and new construction requires expert design and quality materials. We consult on roofing material selection matching architectural style, install complete roofing systems including structure preparation, battens/purlins, underlayment, roofing material, flashings, ridge capping, and gutters. New installations receive our full 10-year guarantee ensuring long-term protection for your investment.

Complete Roof Replacement

Roof replacement becomes necessary when existing roofs reach end of life, show extensive deterioration, or no longer provide adequate protection. We manage complete residential re-roofing projects including removal of old roofing materials, disposal and site cleanup, roof deck inspection and repairs, installation of new roofing system, and upgrades including improved insulation, modern waterproofing, and enhanced ventilation. Replacement projects transform homes with fresh appearance and renewed protection.

Roof Repairs for All Damage Types

Roof repairs address problems before they escalate into major damage and expenses. Common residential repairs include leak repairs identifying and fixing water penetration sources, broken or missing tile replacement, storm and wind damage repairs, ridge capping re-bedding, valley gutter repairs and waterproofing, flashing repairs around chimneys and skylights, and emergency repairs for urgent situations. Prompt repairs prevent interior water damage and extend roof lifespan.

Tile Roofing Installation and Repairs

Tile roofing remains Cape Town's most popular residential choice offering 40-50+ year lifespan, excellent fire resistance, superior thermal insulation, and timeless aesthetics. We install concrete tiles (double Roman, Marseille, slate profile) and premium clay tiles in all colours and profiles. Tile roofing suits traditional and contemporary homes throughout Cape Town's diverse architectural landscape. Our comprehensive tile roofing services include new installation and repairs.

Metal Roofing for Modern and Coastal Homes

Metal roofing provides cost-effective, durable roofing ideal for contemporary homes, coastal properties, and budget-conscious homeowners. We install IBR (Inverted Box Rib) sheeting, corrugated steel roofing, and Chromadek pre-painted steel in various colours. Metal roofing offers 20-40 year lifespan, light weight reducing structural loads, excellent wind resistance, and rapid installation. Particularly suitable for coastal areas due to corrosion-resistant coatings. See our metal roofing services for details.

Slate Roofing for Premium Homes

Slate roofing represents ultimate premium roofing with 75-100+ year lifespan and exceptional beauty. Natural slate tiles provide unmatched durability, fire resistance, zero maintenance, and classic elegance ideal for heritage homes, upmarket properties, and discriminating homeowners throughout Constantia, Bishopscourt, and premium suburbs. We install natural slate and slate profile tiles providing similar aesthetics at lower cost.

Flat Roof Waterproofing

Flat roof waterproofing suits modern home extensions, garages, and contemporary architecture. We install torch-on waterproofing, EPDM rubber roofing, fibreglass GRP systems, and liquid rubber membranes with proper drainage design critical for Cape Town's winter rainfall. Flat roofs maximize ceiling heights and provide clean modern aesthetics. Our flat roof solutions ensure reliable waterproof protection.

Roof Painting and Coating

Roof painting refreshes appearance and extends tile and metal roof lifespan. We provide high-pressure roof cleaning, repairs before painting, primer application, two top coats of premium roof paint, and UV protection. Roof painting costs less than replacement while delivering fresh appearance and renewed weather protection. Ideal for homes 15-25 years old with sound underlying roofs.

Gutter Installation and Repairs

Effective gutter systems protect homes from water damage directing roof runoff safely away from foundations, walls, and landscaping. We install new gutter systems, repair damaged gutters, replace rusted sections, improve drainage capacity, and provide regular gutter cleaning. Proper gutters are essential for Cape Town's winter rainfall preventing foundation undermining and exterior wall water damage.

Roof Inspections and Assessments

Professional roof inspections identify problems early when repairs are simple and affordable. We provide detailed roof condition reports for home buyers, sellers, insurance requirements, or maintenance planning. Inspections include comprehensive condition assessment, photographic documentation, written reports with repair recommendations, cost estimates, and lifespan projections. Annual inspections after major storms catch problems before they cause interior damage.

Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Roofing

How much does a new roof cost for a house in Cape Town?

Residential roof costs in Cape Town range from R67,500-R97,500 for a typical 150m² home with concrete tiles, R82,500-R127,500 for clay tiles, or R52,500-R90,000 for metal roofing depending on materials chosen. Per m² pricing includes R450-R650 for concrete tiles, R550-R850 for clay tiles, R350-R600 for metal roofing, and R450-R750 for flat roof waterproofing. Costs include materials, labour, underlayment, battens/purlins, flashings, ridge capping, and installation. Pricing depends on roof size, pitch complexity, material quality, access difficulty, and height. Contact Murtons Projects on for accurate residential roofing quotes.

What's the best roofing material for Cape Town homes?

The best roofing material depends on your home style, budget, and priorities. Tile roofing (concrete or clay) offers 40-50+ year lifespan, excellent fire resistance, superior insulation, classic appearance, and suits traditional to modern homes. Metal roofing provides 20-40 year lifespan, cost-effectiveness, light weight, various colours, and suits contemporary and coastal homes. Slate roofing delivers premium appearance and exceptional longevity for upmarket properties. Flat roof systems suit modern architecture. All materials perform well in Cape Town's climate when properly installed. Consider architectural style, budget, longevity requirements, and maintenance preferences. Contact Murtons Projects on for personalized recommendations.

How long does residential roof installation take?

Residential roof installation typically takes 3-7 days for average homes depending on roof size, complexity, and weather. Small homes (80-120m²) require 2-4 days, medium homes (120-180m²) take 4-6 days, and large homes (180m²+) need 5-10 days. Timeline depends on roof pitch and complexity (valleys, dormers, chimneys), material type (tiles take longer than metal), weather conditions and delays, removal requirements if re-roofing, and crew size. We provide realistic timelines during quotation and update you on progress. Most installations complete within one week. Call Murtons Projects on for residential roofing timelines.

Do you help with home insurance roofing claims?

Yes, Murtons Projects assists homeowners with insurance claims for roof damage from storms, wind, hail, falling trees, and other insured events. We provide detailed damage documentation with photos, comprehensive damage assessments and reports, accurate repair or replacement quotes for insurers, scope of work descriptions, direct communication with insurance assessors, compliance with insurance requirements and timelines, and completed invoices for claim submission. Our thorough documentation supports successful claims. We understand insurance processes and work professionally with assessors. Emergency repairs prevent further damage while claims process. Call for roofing insurance claim assistance.

Can you match my existing roof tiles?

Yes, we match existing roof tiles for repairs and partial replacements using our extensive supplier network. We identify tile profile (double Roman, Marseille, slate profile, etc.), match colours (terracotta, brown, charcoal, grey, etc.), source matching tiles from current manufacturers, locate reclaimed tiles for discontinued profiles, and suggest alternatives for exact matches unavailable. Cape Town suppliers stock most common profiles. For extensive replacement of discontinued tiles, we may replace larger visible sections ensuring uniform appearance. Quality matching maintains home aesthetics and property value. Contact Murtons Projects on to discuss tile matching for your home.
