Roofing Materials for Parow Homes

Concrete tiles remain the top choice for Parow residential roofing. Concrete tiles suit Parow's climate and provide reliable, long-lasting roofing at prices suitable for middle-income homeowners.

Corrugated Iron and IBR Sheeting

Corrugated iron roofing offers practical, affordable roofing for garages, outbuildings, extensions, and some homes. Options include standard galvanized corrugated iron (most affordable), IBR sheeting (cleaner appearance), and Chromadek pre-painted steel (better durability and appearance). Sheeting provides quick installation, lightweight construction, good water runoff, and easy repairs. Popular for Parow's practical, budget-conscious approach to home maintenance.

Chromadek Roofing

Chromadek pre-painted steel offers upgraded metal roofing with better corrosion resistance, factory-applied color coating, cleaner appearance, and longer lifespan than galvanized iron. Suitable for modern homes, new builds, and quality extensions. More expensive than standard corrugated iron but delivers better long-term value.

Clay Tiles - Premium Option

Clay tiles provide premium roofing for homeowners seeking long-term investment. More expensive than concrete tiles but offer superior durability (70-100 years), better salt resistance, and classic aesthetic. Less common in Parow due to higher cost but excellent for homeowners planning long-term ownership.
