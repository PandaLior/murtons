Types of Roofing Emergencies We Handle

Murtons Projects responds to all emergency roofing situations requiring urgent professional attention:

Severe Roof Leaks

Severe active leaks represent the most common roofing emergency, threatening immediate interior damage. Heavy water flow through compromised roofing damages ceilings (staining, sagging, collapse), interior walls and paint finishes, insulation (reducing effectiveness), electrical systems (safety hazard), flooring and carpets, furniture and possessions, and promotes dangerous mold growth.

We respond immediately to severe leaks identifying leak sources quickly, implementing emergency stopping measures (tarping, patching, temporary sealing), assessing damage extent and repair requirements, and providing permanent repairs as soon as conditions allow. Severe leaks require immediate attention - call 074-141-1494 NOW.

Storm Damage Repairs

Storm damage from Cape Town's winter storms causes extensive roofing problems requiring emergency response. Storm impacts include displaced or missing roof tiles exposing underlayment, torn or lifted metal roof sheeting, damaged ridge capping and flashings, broken roof trusses or battens, debris impact damage (branches, objects), blocked gutters causing overflow, and compromised waterproofing systems.

Post-storm emergency services include immediate roof inspection assessing damage, emergency tarping preventing water ingress, securing loose materials preventing further damage, clearing debris and blocked drainage, and scheduling permanent repairs. Many storm damage repairs qualify for insurance claims - we provide complete documentation supporting your claim.

Wind Damage Emergency Repairs

Wind damage from Cape Town's powerful south-easter winds and winter storms lifts tiles, tears sheeting, and compromises roof integrity. Wind emergencies include lifted or blown-off roof tiles creating large exposed areas, torn or peeled metal sheeting, displaced ridge capping, damaged flashings allowing water penetration, and loose materials posing falling hazards to people and vehicles below.

Our emergency wind damage service includes securing dangerous loose materials, tarping exposed areas, replacing critical missing components, and permanent repairs with enhanced wind-resistant fixing. Coastal and elevated properties face increased wind exposure requiring specialized emergency response. Call 074-141-1494 for urgent wind damage repairs.

Falling Tree and Branch Damage

Falling trees or branches during storms cause severe roof damage requiring immediate emergency response. Tree impact damage includes penetrated or punctured roofing (tiles, sheeting, decking), broken roof trusses or structural members, compressed or damaged roof sections, blocked gutters and valleys, and secondary water damage from compromised weatherproofing.

Emergency tree damage response includes safe tree/branch removal (coordinating with tree services if required), emergency weatherproofing of damaged areas, structural assessment and temporary support if needed, damage documentation for insurance, and permanent structural and roofing repairs. Tree damage often involves insurance claims - we provide comprehensive documentation and work directly with assessors.

Fire Damage Emergency Weatherproofing

Fire damage to roofs from structure fires, grass fires, or nearby fires requires emergency weatherproofing protecting interiors from weather exposure. Fire-damaged roofs may have burned or melted roofing materials, compromised structural integrity, exposed roof decking, and burned flashings and penetrations.

Post-fire emergency roofing includes damage assessment coordinating with fire investigators and insurance, emergency tarping providing weather protection, securing compromised areas preventing further damage, and permanent reconstruction once insurance approves. Fire damage situations are traumatic - we provide compassionate professional service during difficult times.

Structural Failures and Collapses

Structural failures including sagging roofs, collapsed sections, broken trusses, or failing supports represent serious emergencies threatening safety. Structural emergencies require immediate assessment by qualified professionals determining safety, identifying causes (overloading, rot, termite damage, design failures), implementing emergency support or evacuation if dangerous, and permanent structural repairs.

We coordinate with structural engineers when necessary ensuring safe professional response. Structural emergencies demand immediate expert attention - call 074-141-1494 immediately for assistance.
