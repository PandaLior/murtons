Flat Roofing Waterproofing Systems

Murtons Projects installs multiple flat roof waterproofing systems, each suited to specific applications, budgets, and performance requirements:

Torch-On Waterproofing (Modified Bitumen)

Torch-on waterproofing remains Cape Town's most popular flat roof solution, offering proven reliability, cost-effectiveness, and excellent performance. This system uses modified bitumen membranes heat-welded to create seamless waterproof barriers. Multi-layer application (typically 2-3 layers) provides redundancy and long-term protection.

Torch-On Benefits:

  • 10-15 year lifespan with quality materials and installation
  • Cost-effective solution (R450-R650 per m²)
  • Proven track record in Cape Town conditions
  • Excellent adhesion and waterproofing
  • UV-resistant cap sheets available
  • Suitable for most flat roof applications
  • Repairable and maintainable
  • Good thermal properties

Ideal for: Residential extensions, garages, small commercial buildings, budget-conscious projects, and areas without heavy foot traffic.

EPDM Rubber Roofing

EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer) rubber roofing provides premium flat roofing solution with exceptional longevity and UV resistance. This single-ply synthetic rubber membrane offers superior durability, flexibility in temperature extremes, and minimal maintenance requirements. Large sheet installation minimizes seams reducing potential leak points.

EPDM Benefits:

  • 20-30 year lifespan, often exceeding expectations
  • Superior UV and ozone resistance
  • Excellent temperature flexibility (-40°C to +120°C)
  • Environmentally friendly recyclable material
  • Low maintenance requirements
  • Resistant to Cape Town's harsh sun
  • Minimal seams reducing leak potential
  • Easy repairs when needed

Ideal for: Premium residential flat roofs, exposed sunny locations, properties seeking long-term solutions, and environmentally conscious homeowners.

Fibreglass GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic)

Fibreglass GRP roofing creates seamless, durable waterproof surfaces ideal for complex roof shapes, heavy foot traffic areas, and premium applications. Liquid resin and fibreglass matting combine forming single-piece waterproof membrane with no seams or joints. GRP offers excellent strength, chemical resistance, and versatility.

Fibreglass GRP Benefits:

  • 25-30 year lifespan with proper maintenance
  • Completely seamless waterproofing
  • Excellent for complex shapes and details
  • Withstands heavy foot traffic
  • UV-resistant topcoat options
  • Fire-resistant properties
  • Attractive finish available in colours
  • Suitable for balconies and roof terraces

Ideal for: Balconies, roof terraces, complex roof shapes, areas with regular foot traffic, and premium applications requiring seamless finishes.

Liquid Rubber Waterproofing

Liquid rubber waterproofing provides versatile seamless protection ideal for repairs, irregular surfaces, and maintenance applications. Applied as liquid coating, it forms continuous rubber membrane adapting to any shape. Excellent for existing roof restoration and complex details.

Liquid Rubber Benefits:

  • 15-20 year lifespan
  • Seamless application to any shape
  • Excellent for repairs and restoration
  • Bonds to most substrates
  • UV and weather resistant
  • Minimal substrate preparation
  • Fast application and curing
  • Cost-effective for smaller areas

Ideal for: Roof repairs, parapet walls, complex details, small areas, and existing roof restoration. Frequently used in our comprehensive waterproofing services.

Drainage Solutions for Cape Town's Rainfall

Effective drainage is absolutely critical for flat roof success in Cape Town's winter rainfall climate. Our installations incorporate comprehensive drainage planning:

Proper Fall and Slope Design

True "flat" roofs require slight slope (minimum 1:40 fall) directing water to drainage points. We create falls using tapered insulation, screed layers, or structural design. Adequate fall prevents water ponding which accelerates waterproofing deterioration and creates leak risks. Proper slope design is essential for Cape Town's heavy winter rains.

Strategic Drainage Points

We position drainage outlets (roof drains, scuppers, or gutter systems) at lowest points ensuring complete water removal. Adequate drainage point quantity and size prevent overflow during Cape Town's storm events. Regular spacing prevents excessive water travel distance.

Quality Drainage Components

We install proper roof drains with debris filters, correctly sized downpipes handling Cape Town rainfall volumes, overflow provisions for extreme weather, and accessible cleanout points. Quality drainage components ensure reliable long-term performance requiring minimal maintenance.

Gutters and Edge Details

Box gutters, perimeter gutters, and edge flashings must integrate properly with waterproofing systems. We ensure proper gutter sizing for roof area and rainfall intensity, waterproof gutter linings, secure overflow provisions, and accessible maintenance points.

Insulation Options for Flat Roofs

Insulation significantly improves flat roof performance providing thermal comfort and energy savings:

We install rigid insulation boards (polyisocyanurate, extruded polystyrene XPS, expanded polystyrene EPS) beneath waterproofing membranes. Insulation benefits include:

  • Reduced heat gain keeping interiors cooler in Cape Town summers
  • Improved winter warmth reducing heating costs
  • Condensation prevention protecting roof structure
  • Energy cost savings through reduced heating/cooling
  • Enhanced indoor comfort year-round
  • Acoustic insulation reducing noise transmission
  • Increased building value through energy efficiency

Insulation thickness depends on thermal requirements, budget, and building regulations. We recommend appropriate insulation levels for your specific Cape Town property.

Flat Roof Installation Process

Our systematic approach ensures quality flat roof installations:

1. Consultation and Site Assessment

We inspect your property assessing roof structure, drainage requirements, access conditions, and specific needs. We discuss waterproofing system options, insulation possibilities, drainage solutions, and budget considerations. Call 074-141-1494 to schedule your flat roof consultation.

2. Detailed Quote and Planning

We provide comprehensive written quotes specifying waterproofing system, substrate preparation requirements, drainage design, insulation options, material specifications, timeline, and guarantee terms. Detailed planning ensures successful outcomes.

3. Substrate Preparation

Proper substrate preparation is critical. We clean existing surfaces, repair structural damage, create proper fall and slopes, install insulation if specified, prime surfaces for waterproofing adhesion, and ensure dry conditions before waterproofing application.

4. Drainage Installation

We install drainage outlets, edge details, overflow provisions, and gutter systems before waterproofing application. Proper drainage component installation ensures integration with waterproofing membranes.

5. Waterproofing Application

Professional waterproofing installation follows manufacturer specifications and industry best practices. Installation methods vary by system (torch-on welding, EPDM bonding, fibreglass laminating, or liquid application). Multiple layers and proper overlap ensure complete waterproof protection.

6. Edge Details and Flashings

Critical details include upstands at walls and parapets, edge terminations and drips, penetration flashings (vents, pipes), and junction details. Quality detail work prevents leak points.

7. Quality Inspection and Testing

We conduct thorough inspections verifying waterproofing integrity, proper drainage function, complete membrane coverage, and professional finishing. Water testing confirms performance before project completion.

8. Cleanup and Documentation

Complete site cleanup removes all materials and debris. You receive guarantee documentation, maintenance guidelines, and our contact information for future service.

How much does flat roofing cost in Cape Town?

Flat roofing costs in Cape Town range from R450-R650 per m² for torch-on waterproofing to R650-R850 per m² for premium EPDM rubber or fibreglass GRP systems. A typical 50m² flat roof extension costs R22,500-R32,500 for torch-on or R32,500-R42,500 for EPDM/GRP. How long do flat roofs last in Cape Town?

Flat roof lifespan depends on waterproofing system used: Torch-on waterproofing lasts 10-15 years, EPDM rubber roofing 20-30 years, fibreglass GRP 25-30 years, and liquid rubber systems 15-20 years. Lifespan depends on installation quality, drainage effectiveness (critical in Cape Town's rainy winters), maintenance, UV exposure, and foot traffic. Regular inspections and maintenance maximize flat roof longevity. Proper drainage is essential for Cape Town's winter rainfall. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for long-lasting flat roof solutions.

What's the best flat roof waterproofing system?

The best flat roof system depends on your needs: Torch-on waterproofing offers proven reliability and cost-effectiveness for most applications. EPDM rubber provides superior longevity and UV resistance ideal for exposed roofs. Fibreglass GRP delivers seamless waterproofing excellent for complex shapes and heavy foot traffic. Liquid rubber suits repairs and irregular surfaces. For Cape Town's climate, all systems perform well when properly installed with adequate drainage. Budget, expected lifespan, and usage determine the optimal choice. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for expert recommendations.

Do flat roofs leak more than pitched roofs?

Properly installed and maintained flat roofs don't leak more than pitched roofs. Flat roofs rely on waterproof membranes rather than water runoff, making installation quality and drainage critical. Key factors preventing leaks include professional waterproofing installation, effective drainage design (essential for Cape Town rainfall), regular maintenance and inspections, proper detailing at edges and penetrations, and quality waterproofing materials. Flat roofs require more maintenance than pitched roofs but perform excellently when properly managed. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for professional flat roof installation.

Can you install insulation with flat roofing?

Yes, insulation is highly recommended for flat roofs providing thermal comfort, energy savings, and condensation prevention. We install rigid insulation boards (polyisocyanurate, XPS, EPS) beneath waterproofing membranes, providing excellent thermal performance, moisture resistance, and structural support. Insulation thickness depends on thermal requirements and budget. Insulated flat roofs keep homes cooler in Cape Town's hot summers and warmer in winter, reducing heating and cooling costs. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 to discuss insulated flat roof solutions.

How important is drainage for flat roofs in Cape Town?

Drainage is absolutely critical for flat roof success in Cape Town's winter rainfall climate. Flat roofs must have slight fall (minimum 1:40 slope) directing water to outlets, adequate drainage points preventing ponding, properly sized downpipes for heavy rain, regular gutter and drain cleaning, and overflow provisions for extreme rainfall. Poor drainage causes ponding water accelerating waterproofing deterioration and potential leaks. Cape Town's winter storms require robust drainage systems. Professional installation ensures effective drainage. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for expert flat roof drainage design.
