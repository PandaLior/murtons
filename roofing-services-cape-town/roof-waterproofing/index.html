Roof Waterproofing Services

Murtons Projects provides specialized roof waterproofing for all applications requiring waterproof protection:

Flat Roof Torch-On Waterproofing

Torch-on waterproofing using modified bitumen membranes represents Cape Town's most popular flat roof waterproofing solution. Heat-welded installation creates seamless waterproof barriers with proven reliability. We install multi-layer torch-on systems (typically 2-3 layers) providing redundancy and extended lifespan. Torch-on waterproofing suits residential extensions, garages, balconies, commercial flat roofs, and roof terrace applications.

Our torch-on waterproofing process includes thorough substrate preparation and cleaning, primer application ensuring adhesion, base layer installation, intermediate layers for strength, UV-resistant cap sheet, proper detailing at edges and penetrations, and comprehensive quality inspection. Torch-on systems last 10-15 years with proper installation and maintenance. See our comprehensive flat roof solutions for complete flat roofing and waterproofing.

Parapet Wall and Capping Waterproofing

Parapet walls surrounding flat roofs require robust waterproofing protecting vertical surfaces and horizontal cappings from weather exposure. Parapet waterproofing prevents water penetration through walls causing interior damp, protects cappings from water ingress and deterioration, prevents efflorescence and salt damage, seals junctions between roofs and walls, and extends parapet structure lifespan.

We waterproof parapet walls using torch-on membranes wrapping over cappings, liquid rubber systems for complex shapes, acrylic waterproofing coatings, and proper flashings and terminations. Parapet waterproofing is essential for Cape Town buildings with flat roofs preventing costly water damage. Parapets are common leak sources requiring professional waterproofing expertise.

Valley Gutter Waterproofing

Valley gutters where two roof slopes meet channel concentrated water flow making them critical waterproofing points. Valley gutter failures cause severe leaks damaging ceilings and interiors. We provide valley gutter waterproofing using torch-on membranes forming waterproof troughs, modern valley trays and systems, improved flashing and sealing, drainage enhancement preventing blockages, and regular maintenance programs.

Valley gutter waterproofing requires expertise understanding water flow, proper falls, flashing details, and tile integration. DIY valley repairs often fail due to insufficient waterproofing or poor detailing. Professional valley waterproofing ensures reliable performance even during Cape Town's heavy winter rainfall. Valley problems require urgent attention preventing ongoing water damage.

Leak Detection and Repair

Leak detection locates water entry points enabling targeted effective repairs. Water travels along roof decking, trusses, and structures before appearing as interior damage, making leak sources difficult to identify. Our systematic leak detection includes comprehensive roof inspection, water testing simulating rainfall, tracing water paths, thermal imaging for hidden moisture, and identifying waterproofing failures or damage.

Once located, we repair leaks using appropriate waterproofing methods including localized waterproofing membrane application, flashing repairs or replacement, sealant and caulking of penetrations, valley gutter waterproofing, and complete waterproofing renewal if deteriorated. Effective leak repairs require identifying and addressing root causes, not just treating symptoms. Call 074-141-1494 for professional leak detection and repair.

EPDM Rubber Membrane Waterproofing

EPDM rubber roofing provides premium waterproofing solution for flat roofs with exceptional UV resistance and 20-30 year lifespan. Single-ply synthetic rubber membranes minimize seams reducing leak potential. EPDM suits residential flat roofs, commercial applications, areas with extreme sun exposure, and projects requiring long-term waterproofing with minimal maintenance. Higher initial cost delivers superior longevity and performance value.

Liquid Rubber Waterproofing Systems

Liquid rubber waterproofing applies seamless membranes to complex shapes, details, and existing surfaces. Liquid application adapts to any shape creating continuous waterproof barriers. Ideal for parapet walls, complex roof details, repair waterproofing, small areas, and irregular surfaces. Liquid rubber provides 15-20 year lifespan with proper application. Fast application and curing suit urgent waterproofing needs.

Waterproofing Maintenance and Inspections

Regular waterproofing inspections identify problems early when repairs are simple and inexpensive. We provide annual or bi-annual waterproofing inspections checking membrane condition, identifying damage or deterioration, clearing drainage systems, minor repairs preventing escalation, and condition reporting. Proactive maintenance maximizes waterproofing lifespan preventing premature failure and costly replacements.

Roof Waterproofing Process

Our systematic approach ensures effective waterproofing installations:

1. Assessment and Consultation

We inspect your roof assessing waterproofing requirements, identifying existing problems or leaks, evaluating substrate condition, understanding drainage situation, and discussing waterproofing system options. Thorough assessment ensures appropriate waterproofing recommendations. Call 074-141-1494 to schedule your roof waterproofing assessment.

2. Detailed Quotation

We provide comprehensive written quotes specifying waterproofing system and materials, substrate preparation requirements, drainage improvements if needed, timeline and weather considerations, guarantee terms, and itemized pricing. Clear quotations ensure understanding of scope and costs.

3. Substrate Preparation

Proper substrate preparation is critical for waterproofing success. We thoroughly clean surfaces removing dirt, debris, and contaminants, repair cracks, holes, and damage, ensure dry conditions (waterproofing requires dry substrates), create proper falls and drainage, and apply primers ensuring waterproofing adhesion. Inadequate preparation causes waterproofing failures.

4. Drainage Installation or Improvement

Effective drainage prevents waterproofing failures. We install or upgrade drainage outlets and downpipes, create proper falls directing water to drainage, install edge details and flashings, ensure overflow provisions for extreme rainfall, and verify drainage function before waterproofing application.

5. Waterproofing Application

Professional installation follows manufacturer specifications including multiple layers for torch-on systems, proper overlap and sealing, attention to edges and penetrations, quality control throughout application, and curing time before exposure. Weather conditions must be suitable - no rain during installation and curing.

6. Detailing and Finishing

Critical waterproofing details include upstands at walls and parapets, edge terminations and drips, penetration flashings (vents, pipes, equipment), junction detailing with walls and other surfaces, and protective coatings if required. Quality detailing prevents waterproofing failures at vulnerable points.

7. Quality Inspection and Testing

Completed waterproofing undergoes thorough inspection verifying membrane integrity, proper detailing, drainage function, and professional finish. Water testing when appropriate confirms waterproofing effectiveness before project handover.

8. Documentation and Maintenance Guidelines

You receive guarantee documentation, material warranties, maintenance recommendations, and our contact information for future service. Regular inspections and minimal maintenance maximize waterproofing lifespan.

How long does roof waterproofing last?

Roof waterproofing lifespan depends on system type and installation quality: Torch-on waterproofing lasts 10-15 years, EPDM rubber membranes 20-30 years, liquid rubber 15-20 years, and acrylic waterproofing coatings 5-10 years. Lifespan depends on installation quality, Cape Town's UV exposure and weather, drainage effectiveness preventing ponding, foot traffic and maintenance, and proper substrate preparation. Regular inspections and maintenance maximize waterproofing longevity. Proper installation is critical for lifespan. Call Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for professional roof waterproofing.

What's the difference between roof waterproofing and roofing?

Roof waterproofing and roofing serve different purposes. Roofing (tiles, metal sheeting) provides primary weather protection using overlapping materials shedding water. Roof waterproofing uses continuous membranes or coatings creating waterproof barriers essential for flat roofs, parapets, valleys, and leak-prone areas. Waterproofing applications include flat roofs requiring membrane systems not water shedding, parapet walls and cappings exposed to weather, valley gutters channeling high water volumes, underlayment beneath tiles providing backup protection, and leak repairs sealing penetrations. Both work together: pitched roofs use roofing materials with waterproofing underlayment; flat roofs rely primarily on waterproofing membranes. Can you waterproof over existing roof surfaces?

Yes, many waterproofing systems can be applied over existing roof surfaces depending on condition and substrate type. We can waterproof over concrete deck roofs, existing failed waterproofing after preparation, parapet walls and cappings, and valley gutters and flashings. Requirements include proper substrate cleaning and preparation, repairing damage before waterproofing, ensuring dry conditions for application, proper priming for adhesion, and compatibility between old and new materials. Some situations require complete removal of old waterproofing before new application. We assess existing conditions and recommend appropriate solutions. Professional expertise and systematic investigation ensure accurate leak location and effective repairs. Attempting DIY leak repairs often fails due to treating symptoms not sources.
