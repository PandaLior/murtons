Understanding Durbanville's Roofing Challenges

Durbanville's location at the foot of the Tygerberg hills creates unique roofing challenges:

Wind Exposure

Properties in elevated Durbanville areas experience strong winds, particularly during summer. This affects ridge tiles, fascia boards, and loose tiles. We use wind-rated fixings and proper installation techniques to secure roofs against Durbanville's wind conditions.

Temperature Fluctuations

Durbanville experiences significant temperature variations between summer heat and winter cold, causing roof materials to expand and contract. This can crack tiles, loosen fixings, and cause leaks over time. We use flexible materials and proper installation methods to accommodate thermal movement.

Winter Rainfall

Cape Town's winter brings substantial rainfall to the Durbanville wine valley. Proper waterproofing, valley gutters, and drainage are essential. We ensure all roof installations include appropriate waterproofing for Durbanville's rainfall patterns.

Moss and Algae Growth

The wine valley's humidity and tree coverage can lead to moss and algae growth on roofs, particularly on north-facing slopes. We provide roof cleaning, treatment, and preventative coatings to protect Durbanville roofs.
