How Murtons Projects Works: Our Proven 7-Step Process

At Murtons Projects, we believe in transparency, quality, and professionalism at every stage of your roofing, waterproofing, or home improvement project. Our comprehensive 7-step process has been refined to ensure you receive the highest quality workmanship, clear communication, and complete peace of mind from start to finish.

Whether you need roof repairs, complete roof replacement, waterproofing services, or any of our other home improvement solutions across Cape Town, you can trust that every project follows our proven methodology designed to deliver exceptional results that last.

Step 1

Initial Contact - Get in Touch

Your journey with Murtons Projects begins the moment you reach out to us. We make it easy to get in touch through multiple convenient channels:

  • Phone: Call 074-141-1494 to speak directly with our team
  • Email: Send your enquiry to info@murtons.co.za
  • WhatsApp: Message us on 074-141-1494 for quick responses
  • Website Form: Complete our free quote form with your project details

We typically respond within 2-4 hours during business hours (Monday-Friday 7AM-5PM, Saturday 8AM-1PM). For emergency roofing situations, we offer 24/7 availability and can often dispatch a team within hours to assess urgent repairs or leak issues.

During this initial contact, we'll discuss your project needs, answer any immediate questions, and schedule a convenient time for a free site inspection.

Timeline: Immediate response within 2-4 hours
Step 2

Free Site Inspection - Comprehensive Assessment

Our free, no-obligation site inspection is a crucial step that sets the foundation for a successful project. We schedule inspections within 24-48 hours of your initial contact, with same-day inspections often available for urgent situations.

During the site inspection, our experienced professionals will:

  • Thoroughly assess your property and the scope of work required
  • Take detailed measurements and photographs for accurate quoting
  • Identify any underlying issues or special requirements (structural concerns, access challenges, safety considerations)
  • Discuss material options, color choices, and project preferences
  • Answer all your questions and provide expert recommendations
  • Assess site access for equipment and materials delivery

For roofing projects, we'll inspect the roof structure, existing materials, flashing, gutters, and drainage systems. For waterproofing, we'll identify areas of water ingress, test for moisture, and evaluate substrate conditions. This thorough approach ensures nothing is overlooked.

Our inspections are conducted by fully qualified, insured professionals who prioritize safety and attention to detail. You'll receive honest, expert advice on the best solutions for your specific situation and budget.

Timeline: Site inspection within 24-48 hours
Step 3

Detailed Quote - Transparent Pricing

Within 24 hours of your site inspection, you'll receive a comprehensive, itemized quote that provides complete transparency on all aspects of your project.

Your detailed quote includes:

  • Itemized breakdown of all materials, labor, and associated costs
  • Material specifications including brands, grades, colors, and quantities
  • Scope of work with detailed description of all tasks to be completed
  • Project timeline with estimated start date, duration, and completion date
  • Warranty information including our 10-year workmanship guarantee
  • Payment terms and schedule clearly outlined
  • Terms and conditions to protect both parties

We believe in no hidden costs and no surprise charges. Our quotes are comprehensive and cover everything needed to complete your project to the highest standard. If any unforeseen issues are discovered during the work, we'll communicate immediately and provide updated pricing before proceeding.

All quotes are valid for 30 days and come with absolutely no obligation. We encourage you to review the quote carefully, ask questions, and compare with other contractors if desired. We're confident our combination of quality materials, professional workmanship, and competitive pricing offers exceptional value.

Timeline: Quote delivered within 24 hours of inspection
Step 4

Project Planning - Scheduling & Preparation

Once you've approved the quote and we've received your deposit, we move into the detailed project planning phase to ensure smooth execution.

During project planning, we handle:

  • Project scheduling based on your preferences and our team availability
  • Material procurement ordering all specified materials from trusted suppliers
  • Team allocation assigning our best professionals for your specific project type
  • Site preparation planning coordinating access, safety measures, and site protection
  • Permits and approvals if required for your project or location
  • Communication schedule establishing how we'll keep you updated throughout

You'll receive a confirmed start date and a detailed project schedule showing what will happen each day. We'll also discuss any site preparation you can do in advance, such as clearing access routes, relocating vehicles, or protecting sensitive areas.

For roofing projects in Cape Town, we carefully monitor weather forecasts and schedule work during optimal weather windows to ensure quality installation. If weather conditions are unsuitable, we'll reschedule to maintain our quality standards.

We use only premium materials from reputable suppliers, ensuring every component meets or exceeds industry standards. Whether it's concrete roof tiles, metal roofing, waterproofing membranes, or paint products, we never compromise on quality.

Timeline: Project scheduled within 1-2 weeks of approval
Step 5

Professional Installation - Quality Workmanship

This is where our expertise and commitment to quality truly shine. Our fully certified and insured team arrives on time, ready to deliver exceptional workmanship on your roofing, waterproofing, or home improvement project.

During installation, you can expect:

  • Punctual arrival - our team arrives on time every day as scheduled
  • Professional conduct - respectful, courteous service from uniformed professionals
  • Site safety - comprehensive safety measures to protect workers, your property, and occupants
  • Quality materials - only premium products from trusted brands
  • Proven techniques - installation methods that follow industry best practices
  • Daily cleanup - site kept neat and tidy with thorough cleanup at the end of each day
  • Progress updates - regular communication on project status and any developments
  • Property protection - careful measures to protect landscaping, driveways, and adjacent structures

For roofing installations, our team is experienced in working with all roof types - tile roofing, metal roofing, slate roofing, flat roofs, and pitched roofs. We follow manufacturer specifications exactly to maintain warranty validity and ensure optimal performance.

For waterproofing projects, we use proven torch-on, membrane, and liquid rubber systems applied according to strict technical requirements to guarantee watertight results that last.

Throughout the installation, we maintain open communication. If we discover any unexpected issues or opportunities for improvement, we discuss them with you immediately, providing options and recommendations before proceeding.

Safety is our top priority. All team members use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), and we implement comprehensive site safety measures including barriers, warning signs, and fall protection systems for elevated work.

Timeline: Varies by project scope (typically 2-7 days for roofing)
Step 6

Quality Inspection - Final Checks & Approval

Before we consider any project complete, it must pass our rigorous quality inspection process. This comprehensive final check ensures every aspect of the work meets our exacting standards and your expectations.

Our quality inspection includes:

  • Visual inspection of all completed work for quality and finish
  • Technical checks verifying all installations meet specifications and building codes
  • Functionality testing ensuring all systems work correctly (drainage, waterproofing, etc.)
  • Detail review checking flashings, joints, fixings, and finishing touches
  • Site cleanliness comprehensive cleanup and waste removal
  • Client walkthrough showing you the completed work and answering questions

For roofing projects, we inspect every tile or panel, all flashings, ridge capping, valleys, gutters, and downpipes. We check for proper alignment, secure fixing, and weatherproofing. For waterproofing, we verify complete coverage, proper overlaps, and sealed penetrations.

We conduct this inspection together with you when possible, walking through the completed project and explaining what was done. This is your opportunity to ask questions, understand the work performed, and ensure you're completely satisfied.

Only when we're confident the project meets our quality standards and your expectations do we proceed to final sign-off. Your satisfaction is our priority, and we're not finished until you're delighted with the results.

Timeline: Final inspection on project completion day
Step 7

10-Year Guarantee - Your Peace of Mind

Your completed project comes with our comprehensive 10-year workmanship guarantee for roofing and waterproofing work - one of the most comprehensive warranties in the Cape Town home improvement industry.

Your guarantee covers:

  • 10-year workmanship warranty on all roofing and waterproofing installation
  • Material warranties from manufacturers (varies by product, up to 50 years for some materials)
  • Written documentation clearly outlining coverage terms and conditions
  • Responsive service for any warranty claims or concerns
  • Ongoing support maintenance advice and future project consultations

At project completion, you'll receive:

  • Written guarantee certificate with full terms and conditions
  • All relevant manufacturer warranties and product documentation
  • Care and maintenance instructions for your new roof or waterproofing
  • Our contact information for any future questions or service needs
  • Final invoice and proof of payment for your records

Our guarantee is backed by our comprehensive insurance and commitment to customer satisfaction. If any issues arise due to workmanship during the guarantee period, we'll return to address them promptly at no charge to you.

We also provide ongoing support after project completion. Have questions about maintenance? Considering another home improvement project? Need a recommendation for a complementary service? We're here to help, even years after your project is finished.

Many of our clients become repeat customers, returning to us for additional projects or referring friends and family. This loyalty is earned through our commitment to quality work, professional service, and standing behind our guarantees.

Coverage: 10-year workmanship guarantee + manufacturer warranties
