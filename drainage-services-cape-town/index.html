Our Drainage Services

Stormwater Drainage Cape Town

Stormwater drainage systems for managing Cape Town's winter rainfall and preventing property flooding. We install underground pipe systems, catch basins, inspection chambers, and proper outlets. Professional stormwater drainage protects foundations, driveways, and landscaping from water damage. Essential for properties experiencing water pooling, flooding, or erosion during rainy periods.

French Drain Installation

French drains provide effective subsurface drainage for properties with water pooling, soggy lawns, or groundwater problems. These gravel-filled trenches with perforated pipes redirect surface water and groundwater away from problem areas. Ideal for Cape Town properties with poor natural drainage, retaining walls, or basement moisture. We design French drains with correct gradient and proper outlets for optimal performance.

Channel Drains Installation

Channel drains (also called trench drains or linear drains) installed across driveways, patios, and paved areas to intercept surface water. These surface drainage systems prevent water from flowing toward buildings or pooling on paved surfaces. Particularly effective for sloped driveways and properties where surface runoff is problematic.

Gutter Installation Cape Town

Gutter installation and gutter cleaning services throughout Cape Town. We install seamless aluminum gutters, PVC gutters, and steel gutters with matching downpipes to channel roof runoff away from foundations. Proper gutter systems prevent fascia rot, foundation damage, and landscaping erosion. Regular gutter cleaning removes leaf buildup that blocks drainage.

Surface Drainage Systems

Surface drainage solutions including grading, swales, and catch basins to manage water runoff on your property. Surface drainage prevents water pooling, redirects runoff to appropriate outlets, and protects landscaping. Works in conjunction with our paving services and landscaping work.

Underground Drainage Installation

Underground drainage systems using PVC pipes, inspection chambers, and proper gradients to channel water away from structures. Underground drainage is invisible, permanent, and highly effective for comprehensive water management. We install soak aways where appropriate for groundwater absorption.

Erosion Control Solutions

Erosion control for properties experiencing soil loss due to water runoff. We install drainage systems, retaining walls, and vegetation solutions to prevent erosion damage. Critical for sloped Cape Town properties and areas with unstable soil.

Drainage FAQs

How much does drainage installation cost in Cape Town?

Drainage installation costs in Cape Town vary by system type. French drains cost R300-R600 per linear meter, channel drains R250-R450/m, and stormwater drainage R350-R700/m depending on excavation depth and piping size. Gutter installation costs R150-R300/m including downpipes. Final costs depend on system complexity, excavation difficulty, pipe size, and property access. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for a detailed free quote.

How does a French drain work?

A French drain is a gravel-filled trench containing a perforated pipe that redirects surface water and groundwater away from problem areas. Water seeps through gravel into the perforated pipe and drains to a suitable outlet such as a stormwater system, soak away, or natural drainage point. French drains are ideal for Cape Town properties with water pooling, soggy lawns, retaining wall drainage, or basement moisture issues. Proper gradient and outlet are essential for effectiveness.

Do gutters need regular cleaning in Cape Town?

Yes, gutters in Cape Town should be cleaned at least twice yearly, ideally before and after winter. Cape Town's trees, especially during autumn, drop leaves that block gutters and downpipes. Blocked gutters cause water overflow, fascia damage, foundation water penetration, and landscaping erosion. Regular gutter cleaning prevents these issues and extends gutter lifespan. We offer professional gutter cleaning and maintenance services.

What are the signs of poor drainage?

Signs of poor drainage include water pooling after rain, soggy or marshy lawn areas, water stains on foundation walls, basement dampness or flooding, soil erosion, driveway settlement, cracking, and overflowing gutters. Plants may show signs of overwatering or root rot. If you notice any of these issues, contact a drainage contractor for professional assessment and solutions. Call 074-141-1494 for expert drainage solutions.

Can drainage systems prevent foundation damage?

Yes, proper drainage systems are essential for preventing foundation damage. Stormwater drainage, French drains, and gutters divert water away from foundations, preventing soil saturation, settlement, and structural cracking. Cape Town's winter rainfall makes effective water management critical for protecting your property investment. Combined with our waterproofing services, proper drainage provides comprehensive water protection.
