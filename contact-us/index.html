Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

We typically respond within 2-4 hours during business hours (Mon-Fri 7AM-5PM, Sat 8AM-1PM). For emergency roofing issues, we offer 24/7 response and can often have a team on-site within hours.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes! We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all our services. We can often do same-day site inspections and will provide you with a detailed, itemized quote including timeline and warranty information.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all areas within 30km of our Milnerton office, including Northern Suburbs (Durbanville, Table View, Bellville, Parow, Brackenfell), Atlantic Seaboard (Sea Point, Camps Bay, Green Point, Clifton, Hout Bay), Southern Suburbs (Constantia, Claremont, Rondebosch, Newlands), and West Coast (Melkbosstrand, Big Bay, Stellenbosch).

Can I visit your office?

Absolutely! We're located at 10 Northfield Place, Milnerton Rural. You're welcome to drop by during business hours (Mon-Fri 7AM-5PM, Sat 8AM-1PM) - no appointment necessary. We're happy to discuss your project in person.

Do you provide emergency roofing services?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency roofing services for urgent repairs and leak issues. Call anytime and we'll dispatch a team as quickly as possible.

How long does it take to get a quote?

After your initial contact, we typically arrange a free site inspection within 24-48 hours (same-day inspections often available). You'll receive your detailed quote within 24 hours of the inspection.
