About Murtons Projects

Premium Roofing & Home Improvement Specialists Serving Cape Town

Murtons Projects is a premium roofing and home improvement company based in Milnerton, Cape Town, specializing in roofing, waterproofing, painting, paving, drainage, and renovation services for residential and commercial properties throughout the Northern Suburbs, Atlantic Seaboard, and surrounding areas.

Our Story

Recently relocated from Johannesburg, Murtons Projects brings years of proven expertise and premium service standards to Cape Town homeowners and businesses. Our experienced team of professional contractors has extensive experience in all aspects of home improvement, with a particular specialization in roofing services.

Based strategically in Milnerton, we serve all properties within a 30km radius, providing fast response times, same-day site inspections, and emergency services throughout Cape Town's most sought-after suburbs.

Our Mission

To provide premium quality roofing and home improvement services that exceed client expectations, using the highest-grade materials, professional workmanship, and transparent communication at every stage of the project.

Why Choose Murtons Projects?

  • 10-Year Workmanship Guarantee: Industry-leading warranty on all roofing and waterproofing work
  • Fully Insured: Comprehensive liability and workmen's compensation coverage
  • Premium Materials: Only the highest quality products suited to Cape Town's unique climate
  • Professional Team: Experienced, certified contractors who respect your property
  • Transparent Pricing: Detailed quotes with no hidden costs
  • Fast Response: Same-day inspections and emergency services available
  • Local Expertise: Understanding of Cape Town's climate challenges and building requirements

Our Services

We provide comprehensive home improvement solutions including:

  • Roofing Services: Repairs, replacement, tile roofing, metal roofing, emergency repairs
  • Waterproofing: Torch-on, membrane, damp proofing, leak detection
  • Painting Services: Interior, exterior, roof painting, commercial painting
  • Paving Services: Driveways, patios, block paving, concrete paving
  • Drainage Services: Stormwater systems, French drains, gutters, downpipes
  • Renovations: Kitchen, bathroom, extensions, complete home renovations

Our Commitment to Quality

At Murtons Projects, quality is never compromised. We use only premium materials from trusted suppliers, employ experienced tradespeople, and follow industry best practices on every project. Our work is backed by comprehensive guarantees and full insurance coverage for your complete peace of mind.

Service Areas

We serve all Cape Town areas within 30km of Milnerton including:

  • Northern Suburbs: Durbanville, Table View, Bellville, Parow, Brackenfell
  • Atlantic Seaboard: Sea Point, Green Point, Camps Bay, Clifton, Hout Bay
  • Southern Suburbs: Constantia, Claremont, Rondebosch, Newlands
  • West Coast: Melkbosstrand, Big Bay
