Our Waterproofing Services

Roof Waterproofing Cape Town

Roof waterproofing is essential for protecting Cape Town properties from winter rainfall and water damage. We install torch-on waterproofing, APP modified bitumen membranes, liquid rubber systems, and acrylic waterproofing for flat roofs, parapet walls, and low-pitch roofs. Suitable for residential homes, apartments, and commercial buildings.

Torch-On Waterproofing

Torch-on waterproofing using APP modified bitumen membranes provides superior waterproof protection for flat roofs and low-pitch roofs. This heat-applied system creates a seamless, durable waterproof barrier lasting 15-20 years. Ideal for Cape Town's winter rainfall and UV exposure.

Membrane Waterproofing Systems

Membrane waterproofing using reinforced membranes, EPDM rubber, or PVC systems for roofs, basements, and below-grade applications. These flexible systems accommodate building movement and provide long-lasting water ingress protection.

Basement Waterproofing

Basement waterproofing and below-grade waterproofing using cementitious systems, membrane barriers, and drainage solutions. We address rising damp, groundwater penetration, and moisture problems in basements, cellars, and underground parking areas.

Balcony Waterproofing

Balcony waterproofing for apartments and homes throughout Cape Town. We waterproof balconies, terraces, and outdoor entertainment areas using torch-on systems, liquid membranes, or tiles-over-waterproofing solutions. Essential for Atlantic Seaboard apartments and coastal properties.

Damp Proofing Services

Damp proofing and rising damp treatment for walls, foundations, and building structures. We diagnose damp problems, provide moisture barrier installation, and treat existing damp issues with chemical damp proof courses and ventilation solutions.

Leak Detection & Repair

Professional leak detection using advanced equipment to locate hidden leaks in roofs, walls, and foundations. Fast leak repair services for urgent water ingress problems. Available for emergency leak repairs throughout Cape Town.

Parapet Wall Waterproofing

Parapet wall waterproofing and capping waterproofing to protect exposed walls, boundary walls, and roof parapets from water penetration. Critical for preventing structural water damage in Cape Town's rainfall.

Waterproofing FAQs

How much does waterproofing cost in Cape Town?

Waterproofing costs in Cape Town typically range from R150-R300 per square meter depending on the system used. Torch-on waterproofing costs R200-R250/m², membrane systems R180-R280/m², and acrylic waterproofing R120-R180/m². Final costs depend on roof size, accessibility, surface condition, and waterproofing system chosen. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for a detailed free quote.

What is the best waterproofing for Cape Town's climate?

For Cape Town's winter rainfall and UV exposure, torch-on waterproofing and APP modified bitumen membranes are most effective for roofs. For basements and below-grade applications, cementitious waterproofing with membrane systems works best. We assess each property and recommend the most suitable waterproofing system based on your specific needs, budget, and building type.

How long does waterproofing last in Cape Town?

Professional waterproofing in Cape Town lasts 10-20 years depending on the system. Torch-on waterproofing lasts 15-20 years, membrane systems 10-15 years, and acrylic coatings 5-10 years with maintenance. Lifespan depends on proper installation, surface preparation, UV exposure, and maintenance. All Murtons Projects waterproofing comes with a comprehensive 10-year workmanship guarantee.

Can you waterproof during Cape Town's winter?

Waterproofing requires dry conditions for proper adhesion and curing. We can waterproof during winter on dry days between rain periods. For urgent leaks, we provide temporary emergency repairs to prevent further damage, then complete permanent waterproofing when weather permits. We monitor weather forecasts carefully and communicate timing clearly. Call 074-141-1494 for emergency leak repairs.
