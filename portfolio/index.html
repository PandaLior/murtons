Waterproofing Projects

Our waterproofing expertise covers roof waterproofing, balcony waterproofing, basement waterproofing, bathroom waterproofing, and damp proofing using torch-on systems, membrane waterproofing, and liquid rubber applications.

Recent Waterproofing Projects:

  • Balcony Waterproofing - Sea Point - Complete balcony waterproofing using torch-on membrane system with proper drainage slope and tiled finish. Eliminated chronic water ingress issues.
  • Flat Roof Waterproofing - Century City - Multi-story apartment building flat roof waterproofing with liquid rubber system including UV protection and reflective coating.
  • Basement Waterproofing - Camps Bay - External and internal basement waterproofing using membrane system with drainage channel and sump pump installation.
  • Bathroom Waterproofing - Claremont - Pre-tile waterproofing of master bathroom including shower, bath, and floor areas using approved membrane system.
  • Deck Waterproofing - Hout Bay - Timber deck waterproofing using advanced membrane system allowing tiled or timber finish over waterproof layer.
  • Retaining Wall Damp Proofing - Constantia - Retaining wall waterproofing and drainage installation to prevent moisture penetration and structural damage.
Systems Used: Torch-on membranes, liquid rubber, acrylic waterproofing, polyurethane coatings
Applications: Roofs, balconies, basements, bathrooms, decks, retaining walls
Guarantee: 10-year workmanship warranty on waterproofing installations
