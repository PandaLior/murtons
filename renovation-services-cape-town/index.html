Renovation FAQs

How much does a home renovation cost in Cape Town?

Home renovation costs in Cape Town vary widely by scope and finishes. Kitchen renovations cost R80,000-R250,000+, bathroom renovations R50,000-R150,000, and full home renovations R2,500-R5,000 per square meter. Extensions cost R8,000-R15,000/m². Final costs depend on materials quality, finishes selection, structural work requirements, design complexity, and existing condition. Contact Murtons Projects on 074-141-1494 for a detailed free quote tailored to your project.

How long does a renovation project take?

Renovation project timelines vary by scope and complexity. Kitchen renovations take 3-6 weeks, bathroom renovations 2-4 weeks, home extensions 8-16 weeks, and full home renovations 3-6 months. Timeline depends on project size, structural changes required, permit approvals, material availability, weather conditions, and unforeseen issues. We provide detailed project schedules with milestones and keep you informed throughout the remodeling process.

Do I need permits for renovations in Cape Town?

Many renovations in Cape Town require building permits from your local municipality. Structural changes, extensions, plumbing alterations, electrical work, and external modifications typically require permits. Minor cosmetic work like painting or tiling usually doesn't. Permit requirements vary by municipality (City of Cape Town vs. suburban municipalities). We assess permit requirements, assist with applications, and ensure all work complies with Cape Town building regulations and building codes.

Do you offer design services for renovations?

Yes, Murtons Projects offers comprehensive design services including space planning, layout optimization, material selection, fixture selection, colour consultation, and design advice. We work with architects and designers when needed for complex structural changes or formal architectural drawings. Our remodeling contractors provide expert guidance to help you achieve your renovation vision within your budget while maximizing functionality and aesthetics.

Can I live in my home during renovations?

Whether you can live at home during renovations depends on the project scope and your tolerance for disruption. Kitchen and bathroom renovations while living at home is possible but involves inconvenience with temporary loss of facilities. Full home renovations or extensive structural work may require temporary relocation for safety and efficiency. We minimize disruption, work efficiently, maintain clean work areas, and seal off construction zones. Call 074-141-1494 to discuss your specific renovation project and living arrangements.
