Our Painting Services

Interior Painting Cape Town

Interior painting for homes and commercial properties throughout Cape Town. We paint walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinetry using premium water-based acrylic paints. Our professional painters provide meticulous prep work including filling cracks, sanding, and priming for a flawless finish. Suitable for homes, apartments, offices, retail spaces, and commercial buildings.

Exterior Painting Cape Town

Exterior painting using weatherproof paint and UV-resistant coatings designed for Cape Town's harsh climate. We paint exterior walls, fascia boards, window frames, doors, and boundary walls. Thorough surface preparation including pressure washing, paint removal, crack repair, and anti-corrosive primers ensures long-lasting protection against coastal salt air and UV damage.

Roof Painting Cape Town

Roof painting using specialist UV-resistant and anti-corrosive roof coatings for tiled roofs, corrugated iron roofs, and IBR roofs. Our roof painting service includes roof cleaning, rust treatment, leak repair, and application of premium roof sealers. Commercial Painting

Commercial painting for offices, retail stores, warehouses, factories, and commercial buildings. We handle large-scale projects with minimal disruption to business operations. After-hours and weekend painting available. Fast turnaround times and professional project management.

Spray Painting Services

Spray painting using airless spray equipment for fast, even coverage on large exterior areas, warehouses, and commercial properties. Spray painting provides superior finish quality and faster completion times for extensive painting projects.

Professional colour consultation services to help you choose the perfect paint colours for your property. Our painting experts provide advice on current trends, colour psychology, complementary schemes, and colours that enhance Cape Town properties and withstand UV exposure.

Painting FAQs

How much does painting cost in Cape Town?

Painting costs in Cape Town typically range from R35-R80 per square meter depending on the type of work. Interior painting costs R35-R55/m², exterior painting R45-R75/m², and roof painting R40-R70/m². Commercial painting and spray painting vary based on project size and complexity. Final costs depend on surface area, surface condition, number of coats, paint quality, and access requirements. Contact Murtons Projects on for a detailed free quote.

What is the best paint for Cape Town's climate?

For Cape Town's harsh UV exposure, coastal salt air, and winter rainfall, we recommend weatherproof acrylic paint with UV-resistant properties for exteriors. For roofs, UV-resistant and anti-corrosive roof paint is essential to prevent UV degradation and rust. For interiors, water-based acrylic paints provide durability, easy maintenance, and low VOC emissions. We assess each property and recommend the most suitable paint system based on location, exposure, and surface type.

How long does professional painting last in Cape Town?

Professional exterior painting in Cape Town lasts 5-8 years with quality weatherproof paint. Interior painting lasts 8-12 years. Roof painting lasts 5-7 years depending on UV exposure and coastal proximity. Lifespan depends on proper surface preparation, paint quality, number of coats, and maintenance. All Murtons Projects painting includes thorough surface prep, quality materials, and professional application for maximum longevity.

Can you paint during Cape Town's winter?

Painting requires dry conditions for proper adhesion and curing. We can paint during winter on dry days between rain periods. Interior painting can proceed year-round as it's unaffected by weather. For exterior work, we monitor weather forecasts carefully and schedule work during suitable conditions to ensure quality results. We communicate timing clearly and work efficiently on suitable days. Call to discuss your painting project timing.

Do you offer colour consultation services?

Yes, Murtons Projects offers professional colour consultation services to help you choose the perfect paint colours for your home or business. Our experienced painting contractors provide expert advice on colour schemes, current trends, colour psychology, and combinations that suit Cape Town properties, enhance curb appeal, and match your personal style. Colour consultation is included with our painting services.
