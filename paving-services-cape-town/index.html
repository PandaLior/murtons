Block Paving Services

Block paving using concrete paving blocks in various colours, patterns, and finishes. Block paving offers strength, durability, and easy repair. Ideal for driveways, parking areas, and commercial applications. Available in permeable paving options for improved stormwater management.

Concrete Paving

Concrete paving using precast concrete pavers in diverse styles including standard bricks, cobblestone effects, and contemporary designs. Concrete pavers provide excellent load-bearing capacity, UV resistance, and low maintenance. Cost-effective paving solution for large areas.

Brick Paving Services

Brick paving using clay bricks for classic, timeless aesthetics. Clay brick paving offers natural colour variations, durability, and traditional Cape appeal. Suitable for heritage properties, period homes, and clients seeking authentic brick appearance. Excellent for driveways, patios, and walkways.

Interlocking Bricks Installation

Interlocking bricks provide secure, stable paving through mechanical interlocking. These pavers resist displacement, provide excellent load distribution, and allow easy access to underground services. Popular for modern driveways and commercial paving projects.

Paving FAQs

How much does paving cost in Cape Town?

Paving costs in Cape Town typically range from R250-R650 per square meter depending on materials used. Concrete pavers cost R250-R400/m², clay bricks R350-R500/m², and natural stone paving R450-R650/m². Block paving costs R300-R450/m² including installation. Final costs depend on paving area size, excavation depth, base preparation, material choice, and site access. Contact Murtons Projects on for a detailed free quote.

What is the best paving for driveways in Cape Town?

For driveways in Cape Town, concrete pavers and interlocking bricks are most popular due to strength and durability. Concrete paving blocks offer excellent load-bearing capacity, UV resistance, and easy replacement of damaged units. Clay bricks provide classic aesthetics and natural variations. Natural stone offers premium appearance and longevity. We recommend materials based on your budget, aesthetic preferences, vehicle load requirements, and property style.

How long does paving installation take?

Paving installation times vary by project size and complexity. A standard residential driveway (30-50m²) takes 3-5 days including excavation, base preparation, and paving installation. Larger driveways or patio areas may take 1-2 weeks. Complex designs, poor soil conditions, and drainage work can extend timelines. Weather conditions and site access also affect timing. We provide clear timeline estimates during quotation and keep you informed throughout the project.

Does paving require maintenance?

Paving requires minimal maintenance in Cape Town. Regular sweeping and occasional pressure washing keep pavers clean and prevent weed growth. Joint sand may need topping up every 2-3 years to maintain paver stability. Sealants can be applied every 3-5 years for enhanced protection, colour preservation, and easier cleaning. Individual damaged pavers can be easily replaced without disturbing surrounding areas, making block paving very maintenance-friendly.

Can you pave over existing surfaces?

Paving over existing surfaces depends on the condition and type of base. Solid concrete bases in good condition can sometimes be paved over with proper preparation. However, most projects require proper excavation and base preparation for longevity and drainage. We assess each site, test soil conditions, and recommend the best approach for lasting results. Call for a site inspection and professional paving contractor advice.
